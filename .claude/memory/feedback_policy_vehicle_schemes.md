name: feedback_policy_vehicle_schemes
description: Policy notes (GS2/policy/) must always name the implementing scheme(s) that operationalize each policy, not just the policy's stated provisions
metadata:
  type: feedback
---

Every policy note in `notes/GS2/policy/` and `drafts/GS2/policy/` must include a "Vehicle Scheme(s)" section (or clearly labeled equivalent) for each policy version covered — the concrete scheme(s)/mission(s) that operationalize that policy on the ground, not just the policy document's stated goals/targets.

**Why:** Explicit user instruction after building [[Policy on Health]] and [[Policy on Children]] — UPSC answers score higher when they connect a policy to its delivery mechanism (e.g. NEP 2020 → NIPUN Bharat; NHP 2017 → Ayushman Bharat/PM-JAY + AAMs; NHP 2002 → NRHM/NHM; NPC 1974 → ICDS; NPC 2013 → Poshan Abhiyaan/Saksham Anganwadi + Mission Vatsalya; National Policy on Child Labour 1987 → NCLP + PENCIL Portal).

**How to apply:** When drafting or revising any policy note, identify the scheme(s) most directly implementing each policy/sub-policy and give them their own styled callout box (reuse the green `#eaf7ec` "vehicle" callout style established in these notes), including launch year, what it delivers, and key stats where available (from PIB/NITI Aayog/Vision IAS/Drishti IAS/Vajiram & Ravi only, per [[feedback_upsc_source_restriction]]). Do this even if the policy note otherwise looks complete — a policy section without its vehicle scheme is treated as incomplete.
