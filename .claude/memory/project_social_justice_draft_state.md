`notes/GS2/Social Justice.md` is a very large, actively-growing note, fully promoted out of `drafts/` (drafts/ is clean — only folder-structure `.gitkeep`s remain). Structure so far:

- **UNIT 1: Vulnerable Sections and Welfare Schemes for their Protection** — fully built out, sections 1–1.10: Women, Children, Youth, Elderly, SC, ST, OBC, Minorities, PwDs, Transgender Persons.
- **UNIT 2: Issues Relating to Development and Management of Social Sector** — fully built out:
