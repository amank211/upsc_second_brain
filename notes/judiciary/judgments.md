<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px;">Judgments</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eddfc4; border-left:4px solid #c0392b; padding:10px 14px; margin-top:16px; margin-bottom:8px; border-radius:4px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>State of Kerala vs. N.M. Thomas (1975)</b><br>
<span style="display:inline-block; background-color:#c0392b; color:#ffffff; font-size:0.72em; padding:2px 8px; border-radius:10px; margin-top:6px; margin-right:4px;">SC</span>
<span style="display:inline-block; background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; font-size:0.72em; padding:2px 8px; border-radius:10px; margin-top:6px;">GS2</span>
<ul style="margin:8px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>First Supreme Court judgment to use the term 'creamy layer'.</li>
<li>Cautioned against the benefits of reservations being cornered by affluent individuals from backward castes.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#dfe9e5; border-left:4px solid #2c7a6b; padding:10px 14px; margin-bottom:8px; border-radius:4px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Indra Sawhney vs. Union of India (1992)</b><br>
<span style="display:inline-block; background-color:#d68910; color:#ffffff; font-size:0.72em; padding:2px 8px; border-radius:10px; margin-top:6px; margin-right:4px;">OBC</span>
<span style="display:inline-block; background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; font-size:0.72em; padding:2px 8px; border-radius:10px; margin-top:6px;">GS2</span>
<ul style="margin:8px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>A 9-judge bench of the Supreme Court emphasised that the Government must exclude the 'creamy layer' within the OBC category from the benefits of reservations.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eddfc4; border-left:4px solid #c0392b; padding:10px 14px; margin-bottom:8px; border-radius:4px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>CESC Ltd. vs. Subhash Chandra Bose (1992)</b><br>
<span style="display:inline-block; background-color:#16a085; color:#ffffff; font-size:0.72em; padding:2px 8px; border-radius:10px; margin-top:6px; margin-right:4px;">Health</span>
<span style="display:inline-block; background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; font-size:0.72em; padding:2px 8px; border-radius:10px; margin-top:6px;">GS2</span>
<ul style="margin:8px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Supreme Court held that the right to health and medical care is a fundamental right under Article 21, extending to workers' health and well-being.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#dfe9e5; border-left:4px solid #2c7a6b; padding:10px 14px; margin-bottom:8px; border-radius:4px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Paschim Banga Khet Mazdoor Samity vs. State of West Bengal (1996)</b><br>
<span style="display:inline-block; background-color:#16a085; color:#ffffff; font-size:0.72em; padding:2px 8px; border-radius:10px; margin-top:6px; margin-right:4px;">Health</span>
<span style="display:inline-block; background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; font-size:0.72em; padding:2px 8px; border-radius:10px; margin-top:6px;">GS2</span>
<ul style="margin:8px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Supreme Court held that failure of government hospitals to provide timely medical treatment to a person in need violates the right to life under Article 21.</li>
<li>Affirmed that the state cannot cite financial constraints as a defence for denying emergency medical care.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eddfc4; border-left:4px solid #c0392b; padding:10px 14px; margin-bottom:8px; border-radius:4px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Murali S. Deora vs. Union of India (2001)</b><br>
<span style="display:inline-block; background-color:#16a085; color:#ffffff; font-size:0.72em; padding:2px 8px; border-radius:10px; margin-top:6px; margin-right:4px;">Health</span>
<span style="display:inline-block; background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; font-size:0.72em; padding:2px 8px; border-radius:10px; margin-top:6px;">GS2</span>
<ul style="margin:8px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Supreme Court directed a ban on smoking in public places, holding that passive smoking violates the right to health under Article 21 of non-smokers.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#dfe9e5; border-left:4px solid #2c7a6b; padding:10px 14px; margin-bottom:8px; border-radius:4px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>E.V. Chinnaiah vs. State of Andhra Pradesh (2004)</b><br>
<span style="display:inline-block; background-color:#c0392b; color:#ffffff; font-size:0.72em; padding:2px 8px; border-radius:10px; margin-top:6px; margin-right:4px;">SC</span>
<span style="display:inline-block; background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; font-size:0.72em; padding:2px 8px; border-radius:10px; margin-top:6px;">GS2</span>
<ul style="margin:8px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Supreme Court held that sub-classification of SCs amounted to tinkering with the Presidential List, and was therefore violative of Article 341(2), which exclusively vests power in Parliament.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eddfc4; border-left:4px solid #c0392b; padding:10px 14px; margin-bottom:8px; border-radius:4px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>N.D. Jayal vs. Union of India (2004)</b><br>
<span style="display:inline-block; background-color:#16a085; color:#ffffff; font-size:0.72em; padding:2px 8px; border-radius:10px; margin-top:6px; margin-right:4px;">Health</span>
<span style="display:inline-block; background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; font-size:0.72em; padding:2px 8px; border-radius:10px; margin-top:6px;">GS2</span>
<ul style="margin:8px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Supreme Court held that the right to life under Article 21 includes the right to a healthy environment and the right to development, and the two must be balanced.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#dfe9e5; border-left:4px solid #2c7a6b; padding:10px 14px; margin-bottom:8px; border-radius:4px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>NALSA vs. Union of India (2014)</b><br>
<span style="display:inline-block; background-color:#8e44ad; color:#ffffff; font-size:0.72em; padding:2px 8px; border-radius:10px; margin-top:6px; margin-right:4px;">Transgender</span>
<span style="display:inline-block; background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; font-size:0.72em; padding:2px 8px; border-radius:10px; margin-top:6px;">GS2</span>
<ul style="margin:8px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Supreme Court legally recognized transgender persons as a "third gender," affirming their constitutional rights under Articles 14, 15, 16, and 21.</li>
<li>Directed the Centre and States to treat transgender persons as a socially and educationally backward class, entitled to reservations.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eddfc4; border-left:4px solid #c0392b; padding:10px 14px; margin-bottom:8px; border-radius:4px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Jarnail Singh vs. Lachhmi Narain Gupta (2018)</b><br>
<span style="display:inline-block; background-color:#c0392b; color:#ffffff; font-size:0.72em; padding:2px 8px; border-radius:10px; margin-top:6px; margin-right:4px;">SC</span>
<span style="display:inline-block; background-color:#8e44ad; color:#ffffff; font-size:0.72em; padding:2px 8px; border-radius:10px; margin-top:6px; margin-right:4px;">ST</span>
<span style="display:inline-block; background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; font-size:0.72em; padding:2px 8px; border-radius:10px; margin-top:6px;">GS2</span>
<ul style="margin:8px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Supreme Court opined that application of the 'creamy layer' concept to Articles 341 and 342 does not tinker with the Presidential List.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#dfe9e5; border-left:4px solid #2c7a6b; padding:10px 14px; margin-bottom:8px; border-radius:4px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Navtej Singh Johar vs. Union of India (2018)</b><br>
<span style="display:inline-block; background-color:#8e44ad; color:#ffffff; font-size:0.72em; padding:2px 8px; border-radius:10px; margin-top:6px; margin-right:4px;">LGBTQ+</span>
<span style="display:inline-block; background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; font-size:0.72em; padding:2px 8px; border-radius:10px; margin-top:6px;">GS2</span>
<ul style="margin:8px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Supreme Court decriminalized consensual same-sex relationships between adults, striking down part of Section 377 of the IPC as unconstitutional.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eddfc4; border-left:4px solid #c0392b; padding:10px 14px; margin-bottom:8px; border-radius:4px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>State of Punjab &amp; Others vs. Davinder Singh &amp; Others (2024)</b><br>
<span style="display:inline-block; background-color:#c0392b; color:#ffffff; font-size:0.72em; padding:2px 8px; border-radius:10px; margin-top:6px; margin-right:4px;">SC</span>
<span style="display:inline-block; background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; font-size:0.72em; padding:2px 8px; border-radius:10px; margin-top:6px;">GS2</span>
<ul style="margin:8px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Ruled that sub-classification of Scheduled Castes (SCs) is permissible, allowing separate quotas for more backward groups within the SC category.</li>
<li>Affirmed that sub-classification does not violate Article 341(2), as the reservation list is flexible; states must provide empirical data supporting claims of backwardness.</li>
<li>The government has since clarified on multiple occasions that sub-classification shall not be implemented for SCs.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#dfe9e5; border-left:4px solid #2c7a6b; padding:10px 14px; margin-bottom:8px; border-radius:4px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Anjum Kadari &amp; Another vs. Union of India (2024)</b><br>
<span style="display:inline-block; background-color:#2c7a6b; color:#ffffff; font-size:0.72em; padding:2px 8px; border-radius:10px; margin-top:6px; margin-right:4px;">Education</span>
<span style="display:inline-block; background-color:#8e44ad; color:#ffffff; font-size:0.72em; padding:2px 8px; border-radius:10px; margin-top:6px; margin-right:4px;">Minorities</span>
<span style="display:inline-block; background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; font-size:0.72em; padding:2px 8px; border-radius:10px; margin-top:6px;">GS2</span>
<ul style="margin:8px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Supreme Court reversed the Allahabad High Court's judgment that struck down the UP Madarsa Act.</li>
<li>Held that a law can be struck down only for violating Fundamental Rights, not the basic structure.</li>
<li>Confirmed the Act is within state legislative competence under Entry 25, Concurrent List (Education).</li>
<li>Stressed that the Act aims to ensure educational standards and socio-economic inclusion for madarsa students.</li>
<li>Struck down provisions awarding degrees like Fazil and Kamil for violating Section 22 of the UGC Act.</li>
<li>Reaffirmed minority educational rights under Article 30(1) and the role of the NCMEI in safeguarding them.</li>
</ul>
</div>
