<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">1. National Policy on Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS), 2012</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Approved by the Union Cabinet, drafted by the Ministry of Finance's Department of Revenue in consultation with relevant ministries/agencies and State Governments.</li>
<li>Grounded in <b>Article 47</b> of the Constitution, which directs the State to work towards prohibiting the consumption of intoxicating drugs injurious to health, except for medicinal purposes.</li>
<li>Balances tackling drug-related problems with ensuring legitimate medical/scientific availability of narcotic drugs is not disrupted.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Four Broad Focus Areas</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Administration of the NDPS Act and Rules.</li>
<li>Legal production, manufacturing, trade, and use of narcotic drugs/psychotropic substances for medical and scientific purposes.</li>
<li>Illicit (drug) supply reduction.</li>
<li>Illicit (drug) demand reduction — including treatment, rehabilitation, and social reintegration of drug-abuse victims.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "National Policy on Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">1.1. Vehicle Schemes</h3>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyan (NMBA), 2020</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">The policy's demand-reduction and awareness vehicle — a national movement towards a drug-free India.</p>
<ul style="margin:8px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Over <b>10.73 crore people</b> sensitized on substance use, including 3.39 crore youth and 2.27 crore women.</li>
<li>3.29+ lakh educational institutions engaged; 8,000+ trained <b>Master Volunteers</b> expanding reach.</li>
<li><b>345 Integrated Rehabilitation Centres for Addicts (IRCAs)</b> government-supported for treatment, counselling, detoxification, and social reintegration.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>NCORD (Narco-Coordination Centre) Mechanism</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">A four-tier mechanism strengthened in 2019 for coordination between Central and State drug-law-enforcement agencies.</p>
<ul style="margin:8px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>SIMS</b> (Seizure Information Management System) e-portal for trend analysis and trafficking-database management.</li>
<li>Dedicated NCORD Portal for sharing activities/best practices; <b>Anti-Narcotics Task Force (ANTF)</b> established in every state/UT; NIDAAN Portal for narco-offender tracking; canine squads for drug detection.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan"; "Key Initiatives and Achievements for the Year 2021".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">1.2. Key Challenges</h3>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #e6b0aa; border-left:5px solid #a93226; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Punjab's Drug Crisis:</b> ~15.4% of Punjab's population uses narcotics, affecting over 3 million people (PGIMER, 2022) — the epicentre of India's drug-abuse burden.</li>
<li><b>Drone-Based Smuggling Surge:</b> drone incidents along the Pakistan border rose roughly 100-fold in five years — from 3 (2021) to 305 (2025) — showing rising trafficking-network sophistication.</li>
<li><b>Record Seizures:</b> the BSF reported an all-time high of over 1.48 lakh cases and 1,200+ tonnes of narcotics/psychotropic substances seized (2023), reflecting both enforcement intensity and the scale of the inflow.</li>
<li><b>Rehabilitation Capacity Gaps:</b> despite 345 government-supported IRCAs, availability of rehabilitation centres remains inadequate nationwide, and many NGO-run de-addiction centres fail to provide adequate treatment/therapy quality.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Vajiram &amp; Ravi — "Drug Control in India: Vision Document 2026–2029 and NCB Annual Report 2025"; Vision IAS — "Supreme Court expresses concern over the rising drug menace situation in Punjab"; PIB — "Drug Trafficking In The Country".</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Way Forward</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Countering drone-based border trafficking with better surveillance technology, expanding quality-assured rehabilitation capacity beyond the current 345 IRCAs, and sustaining NMBA's demand-reduction awareness push are central to addressing the drug menace's supply and demand sides together.</p>
