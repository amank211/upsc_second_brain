<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">1. National Policy for Skill Development and Entrepreneurship (NPSDE), 2015</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Vision</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">"To create an ecosystem of empowerment by Skilling on a large Scale at Speed with high Standards and to promote a culture of innovation based entrepreneurship which can generate wealth and employment so as to ensure Sustainable livelihoods for all citizens" — meeting the challenge of skilling at scale, speed, standard, and sustainability.</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Key Features</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Provides an <b>umbrella framework</b> for all skilling activities, aligning them to common standards and linking skilling with demand centres.</li>
<li>Addresses obstacles like low aspirational value of skilling, weak integration with formal education, and low-quality training infrastructure/trainers.</li>
<li>Bridges skill gaps via industry engagement, a quality-assurance framework, technology leverage, and expanded apprenticeship opportunities.</li>
<li><b>Equity focus:</b> targeted skilling opportunities for socially/geographically marginalized groups and women.</li>
<li><b>Entrepreneurship component:</b> educates and equips potential entrepreneurs (in and outside formal education), connecting them to mentors, incubators, and credit markets.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "National Policy for Skill Development and Entrepreneurship 2015".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">1.1. Vehicle — Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY) / Skill India Mission</h3>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<p style="margin:0;">PMKVY, launched alongside the policy in 2015, is its principal training vehicle under the broader <b>Skill India Mission</b> umbrella.</p>
<p style="margin:10px 0 0 0;"><b>Achievements by version:</b></p>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>PMKVY 1.0: 19.86 lakh trained.</li>
<li>PMKVY 2.0 (2016-2020): 1.10 crore trained/oriented.</li>
<li>PMKVY 3.0: 7.37 lakh trained.</li>
<li>PMKVY 4.0: 27.24 lakh trained across 38 sectors, 36 States, 737 districts.</li>
<li>Total enrolled since inception (to Oct 2025): ~1.76 crore; total trained: ~1.64 crore.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:10px 0 0 0;"><b>Other outcomes:</b> the share of vocationally trained youth (15–29) rose from <b>7.1% (2017-18)</b> to <b>26.1% (2023-24)</b>; 45% of candidates are women; course offerings expanded into AI, drones, robotics, mechatronics, and IoT.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "10 years of Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana"; Vajiram &amp; Ravi — "Skill India Mission, Registration, Objectives, Features, Achievements".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">1.2. Key Challenges</h3>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #e6b0aa; border-left:5px solid #a93226; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Low Placement Conversion:</b> despite enrolling 31.55 million candidates, only ~18% of PMKVY-trained individuals have secured employment — a major training-to-job disconnect.</li>
<li><b>Weak Employability:</b> India's graduate employability rate stood at just 42.6% in 2024; only 51.25% of assessed young people were found employable (India Skills Report 2024).</li>
<li><b>Industry Mismatch:</b> many courses still focus on traditional trades and syllabus completion, while demand is shifting toward automation and green jobs; India faces a 60–73% demand-supply gap in roles like ML engineer, data scientist, and DevOps engineer.</li>
<li><b>Training Quality Gaps:</b> many ITIs, especially rural ones, lack trained instructors and modern equipment.</li>
<li><b>Urban-Centric Ecosystem:</b> the skilling ecosystem remains largely urban-focused, under-serving the informal and rural workforce.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Vajiram &amp; Ravi — "Rethinking India's Skilling Outcomes"; "Vocational Training in India, Government Initiatives, Challenges"; Drishti IAS — "Bridging India's Skill Gap".</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Way Forward</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Shifting curricula toward automation/green-economy skills, upgrading rural ITI infrastructure and instructor quality, and tightening the training-to-placement pipeline are central to closing the gap between PMKVY's large training numbers and India's still-low actual employability outcomes.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Vajiram &amp; Ravi; Drishti IAS; PIB.</p>
</div>
