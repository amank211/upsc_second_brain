<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">1. National Policy for Persons with Disabilities (NPPD), 2006</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Outlines measures and strategies to protect the rights of persons with disabilities and ensure their full inclusion in society.</li>
<li>Aims to ensure equal opportunities, protection of rights, and full participation of persons with disabilities alongside others.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Key Features</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>All stakeholders expected to ensure equal participation of persons with disabilities in any activity or service intended for the general public.</li>
<li>Calls for greater investment in rehabilitation, support services, and training, including assistive technologies (wheelchairs, hearing aids, white canes) to improve functioning and independence.</li>
<li>Adopts a participatory approach — persons with disabilities to be consulted and actively involved in formulating and implementing policies, laws, and services, per the principle <b>"Nothing about us, without us."</b></li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "Policy for Disabled"; Vision IAS — "Persons with Disability".</p>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">2. Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPWD) Act, 2016 — From Policy to Statute</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<p style="margin:0;">Enacted to give effect to the <b>UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (UNCRPD)</b>, replacing the earlier Persons with Disabilities (PWD) Act, 1995, and converting NPPD 2006's commitments into enforceable law.</p>
<p style="margin:10px 0 0 0;">Expanded the definition of disability from <b>7 to 21 categories</b>; defines a "person with benchmark disability" as one with not less than <b>40%</b> of a specified disability.</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Key Features</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Prohibits discrimination against persons with disabilities across employment, education, and healthcare.</li>
<li>Emphasizes dignity, non-discrimination, and inclusion as statutory rights, not just policy aspirations.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Vision IAS — "Rights of Persons with Disabilities (RPWD) Act, 2016"; Vajiram &amp; Ravi.</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">2.1. Vehicle Scheme — Accessible India Campaign (Sugamya Bharat Abhiyan)</h3>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<p style="margin:0;">Launched <b>3 December 2015</b>, targeting universal accessibility across three domains: <b>built infrastructure, transport systems, and the ICT ecosystem</b>.</p>
<p style="margin:10px 0 0 0;"><b>Achievements (over ~9 years):</b></p>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>1,630</b> government buildings (1,030 Central) made accessible.</li>
<li><b>35</b> international and <b>55</b> domestic airports made accessible; all 709 A1/A/B category railway stations given Short Term accessibility facilities.</li>
<li><b>~29%</b> of buses made partially accessible, <b>~5.7%</b> fully accessible.</li>
<li><b>627</b> Central/State/UT government websites made accessible.</li>
<li>Budget rose from <b>₹560 crore (2013-14)</b> to <b>₹1,225.15 crore (2023-24 BE)</b>, with ₹1,143.89 crore spent — the highest in a decade.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "Accessible India Campaign — An Inclusive Society Creates a Sashakt Bharat"; Drishti IAS — "Sugamya Bharat Abhiyan".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">2.2. Key Challenges</h3>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #e6b0aa; border-left:5px solid #a93226; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Underfunding:</b> the Parliamentary Standing Committee (2022-23) found the budget for the Scheme for Implementation of the RPWD Act (SIPDA) grew by less than 9% between 2016-17 and 2020-21, despite expanding programme components.</li>
<li><b>Rehabilitation Infrastructure Gap:</b> a 2024 CAG audit found only 264 of a targeted 310 District Disability Rehabilitation Centres (DDRCs) were functional nationwide.</li>
<li><b>Employment Gap:</b> nearly 37.9% of surveyed companies employ zero persons with disabilities on a permanent basis.</li>
<li><b>Institutional Gaps:</b> only 23 of 35 states/UTs had constituted the mandated State Advisory Boards on disability; several states (Andhra Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh) had not appointed State Commissioners.</li>
<li><b>Implementation-Execution Gap:</b> progressive legislation is undermined by weak fiscal backing and administrative will — rehabilitation centres and specialized courts remain largely on paper as states fail to notify rules or allocate budgets.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Drishti IAS — "Building a Barrier-Free India for Divyangjan"; "PwDs in India: From Margins to Mainstream"; Vision IAS.</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Way Forward</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Closing the gap between the RPWD Act's statutory guarantees and ground realities requires sustained SIPDA funding, faster DDRC rollout, mandatory State Advisory Board/Commissioner appointments, and stronger enforcement of employment and accessibility obligations on both public and private employers.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Drishti IAS; Vision IAS; PIB.</p>
</div>
