<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">1. National Manufacturing Policy (NMP), 2011</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Notified on <b>4 November 2011</b>, with the objective of raising manufacturing's share of GDP to <b>25%</b> and creating <b>100 million jobs</b> over a decade or so.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Key Interventions</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Industrial infrastructure development.</li>
<li>Improving the business environment via rationalization and simplification of business regulations.</li>
<li>Development of appropriate — especially green — technologies for sustainable manufacturing.</li>
<li>Skill development of the younger population.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "National Manufacturing Policy".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">1.1. Vehicle — National Investment and Manufacturing Zones (NIMZs)</h3>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<p style="margin:0;">NMP 2011's industrial-infrastructure vehicle — large, integrated industrial townships with state-of-the-art infrastructure, zoning-based land use, and clean/energy-efficient technologies.</p>
<p style="margin:10px 0 0 0;"><b>NIMZ vs. SEZ:</b> SEZs are export-promotion focused; NIMZs are built on industrial growth in partnership with States, focused on manufacturing growth and employment generation — differing from SEZs in size, infrastructure planning, and governance.</p>
<p style="margin:10px 0 0 0;"><b>Status:</b> only <b>3 NIMZs</b> — Prakasam (Andhra Pradesh), Sangareddy (Telangana), Kalinganagar (Odisha) — received final approval, out of 16 proposed; 13 more had only in-principle approval.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "Establishment of NIMZs"; "16 National Investment and Manufacturing Zones(NIMZS) to Boost Manufacturing Sector".</p>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">2. Later Vehicles — Make in India &amp; the PLI Scheme</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Make in India, 2014</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">A broader flagship initiative launched to boost domestic manufacturing, ease of doing business, and foreign investment — effectively superseding NIMZs as the government's primary manufacturing-promotion narrative.</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Production Linked Incentive (PLI) Scheme, 2020</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Covers <b>14 strategic sectors</b> (mobile/electronics, pharma/APIs, medical devices, autos, specialty steel, telecom, white goods, food processing, textiles, solar PV, ACC batteries, drones, etc.) with a total outlay of <b>₹1.97 lakh crore</b>, aligned with Atmanirbhar Bharat.</p>
<p style="margin:10px 0 0 0;"><b>Achievements (as of Dec 2025):</b></p>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Cumulative investment &gt; <b>₹2.16 lakh crore</b>; cumulative sales &gt; ₹20.41 lakh crore; cumulative exports &gt; ₹8.3 lakh crore.</li>
<li><b>836 applications</b> approved across the 14 sectors; <b>14.39 lakh</b> direct and indirect jobs generated.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "755 applications approved across 14 sectors..."; "PLI Scheme: Powering India's Industrial Renaissance"; Vision IAS — "Production Linked Incentive (PLI) Scheme".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">2.1. Key Challenges</h3>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #e6b0aa; border-left:5px solid #a93226; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Core Targets Missed:</b> manufacturing's share of GDP has never reached NMP 2011's 25% goal, remaining stuck around <b>16–17%</b> for decades and recently ceding further ground to services; the 100-million-jobs and 12–14% annual growth targets were also not met.</li>
<li><b>NIMZ Underperformance:</b> only 3 of 16 proposed NIMZs secured final approval — the flagship industrial-infrastructure vehicle largely stalled.</li>
<li><b>High Logistics Costs:</b> logistics costs run 14–18% of GDP in India versus 8–10% in developed economies; India ranked 38th on the World Bank's 2023 Logistics Performance Index.</li>
<li><b>Unstable Investment Climate:</b> frequent trade/taxation policy changes, burdensome regulations, and complex land-acquisition laws continue to delay projects.</li>
<li><b>Assembly-Led, Low-Employment Growth:</b> manufacturing expansion has been capital-intensive and assembly-led, with weak domestic value chains, low R&amp;D, limited MSME scaling, and low employment elasticity — even where PLI investment has landed.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Drishti IAS — "Assessing Make in India: Achievements and Challenges"; "Reforming India's Manufacturing Sector"; Vajiram &amp; Ravi — "Structural and Policy Constraints to Manufacturing in India".</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Way Forward</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Bringing down logistics costs, simplifying land acquisition and regulatory compliance, and deepening domestic value chains (beyond assembly-stage PLI investment) are central to converting India's PLI-era investment gains into the manufacturing-GDP share NMP 2011 originally targeted.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Drishti IAS; Vajiram &amp; Ravi; PIB.</p>
</div>
