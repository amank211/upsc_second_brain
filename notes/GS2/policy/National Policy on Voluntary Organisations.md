<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">1. National Policy on the Voluntary Sector, 2007</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Approved by the Union Cabinet, structured in six parts: Preamble, Scope of the Policy, Objectives of the Policy, Establishing an Enabling Environment, Partnership in Development, and Strengthening the Voluntary Sector.</li>
<li>Aims to give Voluntary Organisations (VOs) an enabling environment to operate effectively while also making them transparent and accountable, so they can play a constructive role in development.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Four Objectives</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Create an enabling environment for VOs that stimulates their effectiveness while protecting their identity and autonomy.</li>
<li>Enable VOs to legitimately mobilize necessary financial resources from India and abroad.</li>
<li>Identify systems by which Government and the Voluntary Sector can work together.</li>
<li>Encourage VOs to adopt transparent and accountable systems of governance and management.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "National Policy on the Voluntary Sector"; NITI Aayog.</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Key Features</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Enabling Environment:</b> commits to examining the feasibility of a simple, liberal central law to register societies, trusts, and non-profit companies.</li>
<li><b>Partnership in Development:</b> recognizes and elaborates three instruments of partnership between Government and the voluntary sector.</li>
<li><b>Strengthening the Voluntary Sector:</b> commits to encouraging independent philanthropic institutions to fund deserving VOs.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB; NITI Aayog.</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">1.1. Vehicle Scheme &amp; Regulatory Backbone</h3>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>NGO Darpan Portal — NITI Aayog's Voluntary Action Cell (VAC)</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>An e-governance platform providing an interface between NGOs/VOs and key government ministries/departments/bodies.</li>
<li>Issues a unique <b>Darpan ID</b> to registered NPOs — mandatory for any NPO seeking grants from Government of India ministries/departments.</li>
<li>Currently records nearly <b>6 lakh</b> voluntary organisations, of which only about <b>14,500</b> hold active FCRA registration.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: NITI Aayog — "Voluntary Action Cell"; Drishti IAS.</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act (FCRA), 2010</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">The legal instrument regulating how VOs mobilize and use foreign contributions — the "legitimate resource mobilization" objective of the 2007 policy is operationalized through FCRA registration and compliance, administered by the Ministry of Home Affairs.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "FCRA: Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act"; Vajiram &amp; Ravi.</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">1.2. Key Challenges</h3>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #e6b0aa; border-left:5px solid #a93226; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Mass FCRA License Cancellations:</b> over <b>20,701 licenses</b> have been revoked since FCRA's inception for alleged violations, sharply narrowing the pool of VOs eligible for foreign funding (only ~14,500 of ~6 lakh registered VOs currently hold active FCRA registration).</li>
<li><b>Tightening Compliance Burden:</b> VOs must utilize at least 75% of previous funds before receiving further installments, and the permissible administrative-expense limit on foreign grants was slashed from 50% to 20% — squeezing operational capacity.</li>
<li><b>Domestic Funding Skew:</b> CSR funds are largely directed toward corporate-linked NGOs, leaving smaller, grassroots VOs underfunded even as foreign funding routes tighten.</li>
<li><b>Concerns Over Overreach:</b> recent FCRA Amendment Rules add activity-specific registration, restrictions on foreign key functionaries, and a "designated authority" empowered to control assets if a licence is suspended/cancelled — raising concerns about shrinking civil society space, contrary to the 2007 policy's stated commitment to protect VO autonomy.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Drishti IAS — "Crackdown on NGOs under FCRA, 2010"; "FCRA Amendment Rules 2026"; Vision IAS — "FCRA changes risk expanding state control over civil society space"; Vajiram &amp; Ravi.</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Way Forward</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Balancing legitimate national-security and transparency concerns against the 2007 policy's founding promise of an "enabling environment" for VOs remains the central tension — particularly as FCRA compliance rules tighten faster than the voluntary sector's own accountability and diversified-funding capacity can adapt.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Drishti IAS; Vision IAS; PIB.</p>
</div>
