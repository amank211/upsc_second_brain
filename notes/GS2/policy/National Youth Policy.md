<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">1. National Youth Policy, 1988</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>India's first structured National Youth Policy, stressing the role of youth in national development.</li>
<li>Defined "youth" as the age group <b>15–35 years</b> — the widest definition across all three policies.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Key Features</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Focused on personality development and skill development as the primary route to channeling youth energy.</li>
<li>Treated youth development largely as a welfare/character-building concern rather than an economic or rights-based one.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Drishti IAS; Vajiram &amp; Ravi.</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">1.1. Vehicle Scheme — Nehru Yuva Kendra Sangathan (NYKS), 1987-88</h3>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<p style="margin:0;">Set up in <b>1987-88</b> as an autonomous organization under the Ministry of Youth Affairs &amp; Sports, alongside the 1988 policy — the largest grassroots-level youth organization of its kind in the world.</p>
<ul style="margin:8px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Mobilizes rural youth into local <b>Youth Clubs</b> for community development, awareness campaigns, and nation-building activities.</li>
<li>Today covers <b>50.34 lakh youth volunteers</b> through <b>2.57 lakh Youth Clubs</b> across <b>623 districts</b>.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "Nehru Yuva Kendra Sangathan"; "National Youth Day 2025".</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #e6b0aa; border-left:5px solid #a93226; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Key Challenges</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>A very wide 15–35 age band blurred the distinction between adolescents, working-age adults, and mid-career individuals, diluting targeted intervention.</li>
<li>Narrow personality/skill-development framing left out employment, health, and governance-participation dimensions that later policies had to add.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">2. National Youth Policy, 2003</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Replaced NYP 1988; redefined youth as <b>13–35 years</b>.</li>
<li>Aimed to promote patriotism, social justice, and national integration among the youth.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Four Thrust Areas</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Youth Empowerment.</li>
<li>Gender Justice.</li>
<li>Inter-Sectoral Approach.</li>
<li>Information and Research Network.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Key Sectors of Youth Development</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Education; Training and Employment; Health; Environment; Sports and Recreation; Arts and Culture; Science and Technology; Civics and Citizenship.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "National Youth Policy, 2003".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">2.1. Vehicle Scheme — National Service Scheme (NSS)</h3>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<p style="margin:0;">Launched in <b>1969</b> (pre-dating the 1988/2003 policies, but expanded as their community-service delivery vehicle) — mobilizes student volunteers for social and community service.</p>
<p style="margin:10px 0 0 0;">Today active in over <b>700 universities</b>, <b>751</b> higher-secondary councils/directorates, <b>20,254</b> colleges/technical institutions, and <b>11,767</b> schools.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "Nehru Yuva Kendra Sangathan and National Service Scheme observe Shaheed Diwas...".</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #e6b0aa; border-left:5px solid #a93226; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Key Challenges</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Eight distinct sectors and four thrust areas, spread across many ministries/departments, made inter-sectoral coordination difficult in practice.</li>
<li>Did not anticipate the scale of the coming demographic dividend or the digital/skilling economy that would define youth concerns by the 2010s.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">3. National Youth Policy (NYP), 2014</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<p style="margin:0;">NYP 2014 replaced NYP 2003 and is India's <b>current</b> National Youth Policy. It narrows the definition of "youth" to <b>15–29 years</b>, aligning with the age band actually used by most youth-targeted schemes.</p>
<p style="margin:10px 0 0 0;">Vision: to empower the youth of the country to achieve their full potential, and through them enable India to find its rightful place in the community of nations.</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">3.1. Five Objectives</h3>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Creating a productive workforce.</li>
<li>Developing a healthy generation.</li>
<li>Instilling social values.</li>
<li>Facilitating youth participation in governance.</li>
<li>Supporting disadvantaged youth.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">3.3. Vehicle Schemes</h3>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Rashtriya Yuva Sashaktikaran Karyakram (RYSK)</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">The flagship Central Sector umbrella scheme of the Ministry of Youth Affairs &amp; Sports operationalizing NYP 2014, currently approved for <b>2021-22 to 2025-26</b> with a budget of <b>₹2,710.65 crore</b>. Beneficiaries are youth aged 15–29, matching the NYP 2014 definition.</p>
<p style="margin:10px 0 0 0;"><b>Sub-schemes under RYSK:</b></p>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Nehru Yuva Kendra Sangathan (NYKS)</b> — the grassroots youth-club network carried over from NYP 1988.</li>
<li><b>National Youth Corps (NYC)</b> — implemented through NYKS; an amalgamation of the earlier National Service Volunteer (NSV) and Rashtriya Sadbhawana Yojana (RSY) volunteer schemes.</li>
<li><b>National Programme for Youth &amp; Adolescent Development (NPYAD)</b>, International Cooperation, Youth Hostels, Assistance to Scouting &amp; Guiding Organizations, National Discipline Scheme (NDS), and National Young Leaders Programme (NYLP).</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "Rashtriya Yuva Sashaktikaran Karyakram Scheme..."; Drishti IAS.</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Mera Yuva Bharat (MY Bharat), 2023</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Launched by the Prime Minister on 31 October 2023 (National Unity Day); an autonomous body approved by the Union Cabinet as a "phygital" (physical + digital) platform for youth-led development.</p>
<ul style="margin:8px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Provides a single "whole-of-government" framework for learning, volunteering, mentorship, career services, and civic participation.</li>
<li>Since 2023, both <b>NSS</b> (NYP 2003's vehicle) and <b>NYKS</b> (NYP 1988's vehicle) function as part of the MY Bharat framework — bringing all three policies' delivery vehicles under one platform.</li>
<li>Over <b>2 crore youth</b> registered on the platform.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "Prime Minister launches 'Mera Yuva Bharat (MY Bharat)' platform"; "MY BHARAT CROSSES 2 CRORE REGISTRATIONS"; Vajiram &amp; Ravi.</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">3.6. Key Challenges</h3>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #e6b0aa; border-left:5px solid #a93226; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Youth Unemployment/Skill Mismatch:</b> India faces a severe youth employment crisis marked by high unemployment and underemployment among the educated, especially young women, with a persistent mismatch between education and job-market needs.</li>
<li><b>Policy Overtaken by Events:</b> NYP 2014 predates the scale of India's digital and gig economy and the post-pandemic shift in youth aspirations, leaving a gap between the policy text and current youth concerns.</li>
<li><b>Fragmentation Despite RYSK/MY Bharat:</b> Youth development still spans multiple ministries (skilling, education, labour, sports); RYSK and MY Bharat converge the Youth Affairs Ministry's own schemes but not the wider inter-ministerial landscape.</li>
<li><b>Draft Revision Still Pending:</b> A draft revised National Youth Policy has been placed in the public domain (a 2021 draft, and more recently a proposal aligned with <b>Viksit Bharat 2047</b>/SDG 2030 timelines) — but NYP 2014 remains the only <i>adopted</i> policy, echoing the still-pending status seen with the [[National Policy for Women]] draft.</li>
</ul>

<b>Way Forward</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Finalizing the pending policy revision, tightening inter-ministerial convergence beyond RYSK/MY Bharat, and closing the education-to-employment skill gap are central to converting India's demographic dividend into productive youth outcomes.</p>
