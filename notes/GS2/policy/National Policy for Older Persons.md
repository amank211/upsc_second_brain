<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">1. National Policy on Older Persons (NPOP), 1999</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Announced in 1999 to reaffirm the Government's commitment to the well-being of older persons — India's first, and still only formally adopted, National Policy on Older Persons.</li>
<li>Envisages State support to ensure financial and food security, health care, shelter, and other needs of older persons.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Key Features</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>An equitable share in development for older persons.</li>
<li>Protection against abuse and exploitation.</li>
<li>Availability of services to improve the quality of older persons' lives.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "National Policy for Older Persons".</p>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">2. Maintenance and Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens (MWPSC) Act, 2007</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<p style="margin:0;">The legislative backbone giving NPOP 1999's protection commitments legal teeth — passed to ensure need-based maintenance and welfare for parents and senior citizens.</p>
<ul style="margin:8px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Makes maintenance of parents/senior citizens by children/relatives <b>obligatory and justiciable</b> through Tribunals.</li>
<li>Allows <b>revocation of property transfer</b> by a senior citizen where a relative subsequently neglects them.</li>
<li>Introduces a <b>penal provision for abandonment</b> of senior citizens.</li>
<li>Mandates State Governments to establish <b>Old Age Homes</b> for indigent senior citizens, with adequate medical facilities and security.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB; Drishti IAS — "National Policy on Older Persons in India".</p>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">3. Draft National Policy on Senior Citizens (NPSC), 2011</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<p style="margin:0;">A Committee submitted the draft NPSC on <b>30 March 2011</b>, meant to revise NPOP 1999 for a changed demographic reality — but it has never been formally adopted, leaving NPOP 1999 as India's only official policy on the subject, even as a newer "National Policy for Senior Citizens" review has been discussed since.</p>
<p style="margin:10px 0 0 0;"><b>Priorities:</b> accords priority to citizens aged 80+, elderly women, and the rural poor.</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Key Objectives</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Mainstream the concerns of senior citizens, especially older women.</li>
<li>Promote income-security and healthcare-insurance schemes.</li>
<li>Promote care within the family, treating institutional care as a last resort.</li>
<li>Work towards an age-friendly society and recognize senior citizens as a valuable resource.</li>
<li>Promote long-term savings instruments.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "Review of National Policy for Senior Citizens"; Vajiram &amp; Ravi — "India's New National Policy for Senior Citizens".</p>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">4. Vehicle Schemes</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana (AVYAY), 2021</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">The Ministry of Social Justice &amp; Empowerment's revamped umbrella scheme for senior citizens (since April 2021), subsuming earlier elderly-welfare schemes.</p>
<ul style="margin:8px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Integrated Programme for Senior Citizens (IPSrC):</b> supports 552 Senior Citizen Homes, 14 Continuous Care Homes, 19 mobile Medicare units, and 5 physiotherapy clinics via NGOs, with 639 projects funded nationwide.</li>
<li><b>Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana (RVY):</b> distributes assistive devices — 8,48,841 devices to 1,57,514 beneficiaries across 130+ camps.</li>
<li>₹165.50 crore released to implementing organizations over the last two years.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana: Empowering the Elderly for a Dignified Life".</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>SACRED Portal</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">"Senior Able Citizens for Re-Employment in Dignity" — a portal connecting senior citizens seeking re-employment with employers, operationalizing NPOP's "senior citizens as a valuable resource" principle.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "Senior Able Citizens for Re Employment in Dignity (SACRED) Portal".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">4.1. Key Challenges</h3>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #e6b0aa; border-left:5px solid #a93226; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Policy Stuck in Draft:</b> the 2011 revision was never formally adopted — India's elderly-welfare policy still formally rests on a 1999 document, echoing the same "adopted-but-stale" pattern seen with the [[National Policy for Women]] draft.</li>
<li><b>Rapid Demographic Shift:</b> the senior-citizen share of the population is projected to rise from 8.23% (2011) to 12.16% (2026) and 20% by 2047 — a scale the 1999 policy did not anticipate.</li>
<li><b>Feminisation of Ageing:</b> between 2000 and 2050, the 80-plus population is projected to grow 700%, dominated by widowed, highly dependent very old women — a group the 2011 draft flagged as a priority but which remains under-served.</li>
<li><b>Healthcare Accessibility Gaps:</b> limited geriatric care facilities, lack of regular health check-ups, and high out-of-pocket expenditure remain persistent concerns for the elderly.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Vajiram &amp; Ravi — "India's New National Policy for Senior Citizens"; NITI Aayog — "Reforms in India Senior Care".</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Way Forward</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Finalizing a revised National Policy for Senior Citizens attuned to today's demographic scale, expanding geriatric healthcare and long-term care insurance, and targeting support at the fastest-growing, most vulnerable segment — very old, widowed women — are central to closing the gap between NPOP 1999's promises and 2047-era realities.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: NITI Aayog; Vajiram &amp; Ravi; PIB.</p>
</div>
