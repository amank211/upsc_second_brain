<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">1. National Digital Communications Policy (NDCP), 2018</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Cabinet-approved policy envisioning a ubiquitous, resilient, secure, accessible, and affordable Digital Communications Infrastructure to meet citizens' and enterprises' information needs.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Key Objectives (by 2022)</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Universal broadband connectivity at <b>50 Mbps</b> to every citizen.</li>
<li><b>1 Gbps</b> connectivity to all Gram Panchayats (10 Gbps by 2022), extending to all uncovered areas.</li>
<li>Attract <b>USD 100 billion</b> in investment into the digital communications sector.</li>
<li>Establish a comprehensive data-protection regime safeguarding privacy, autonomy, and choice.</li>
<li>Enforce accountability through institutional mechanisms for safe, secure digital communications.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "National Digital Communication Policy, 2018"; Drishti IAS.</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">1.1. Vehicle — BharatNet (with GramNet &amp; NagarNet)</h3>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<p style="margin:0;">The world's largest rural broadband programme — NDCP 2018's principal delivery vehicle for universal connectivity. Complemented by <b>GramNet</b> (connects rural development institutions at 10 Mbps, upgradeable to 100 Mbps) and <b>NagarNet</b> (1 million public Wi-Fi hotspots in urban areas).</p>
<p style="margin:10px 0 0 0;"><b>Amended BharatNet (2023):</b> ₹1,39,579 crore approved to extend broadband to all <b>2.64 lakh Gram Panchayats</b> and ~3.8 lakh villages on demand, across 28 States and 8 UTs.</p>
<p style="margin:10px 0 0 0;"><b>Achievements:</b> 2,18,347 GPs made service-ready (as of March 2025); Optical Fibre Cable network at 42.13 lakh route km; over 14 lakh Fibre-to-the-Home (FTTH) connections delivered against a 1.5 crore target.</p>
<p style="margin:10px 0 0 0;"><b>BharatNet Udyami Model:</b> 7,570 BharatNet Udyamis (entrepreneurs) selected to drive demand-led last-mile connectivity to the remaining 3.8 lakh+ villages, with capital/operational subsidies.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "BharatNet: Bridging the Digital Divide"; Vajiram &amp; Ravi — "BharatNet Project | Objectives, Progress &amp; Challenges".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">1.2. Key Challenges</h3>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #e6b0aa; border-left:5px solid #a93226; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>PPP Model Stalled:</b> the Public-Private-Partnership model approved by Cabinet in June 2021 has yet to take off; the first RFP floated in July 2021 got no private-sector response, forcing the pivot to the Udyami model.</li>
<li><b>2022 Targets Slipped:</b> universal 50 Mbps broadband and 1 Gbps-per-Gram-Panchayat goals were not achieved by their original 2022 deadline; BharatNet coverage is still being extended years later.</li>
<li><b>Multi-Dimensional Bottlenecks:</b> the project faces a mix of technical, financial, operational, and awareness-related challenges in reaching the remotest villages.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Vajiram &amp; Ravi — "BharatNet Project | Objectives, Progress &amp; Challenges"; Drishti IAS — "BharatNet Project".</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Way Forward</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Scaling the demand-driven BharatNet Udyami model, resolving the stalled PPP framework, and sustaining FTTH rollout are key to closing the gap between NDCP 2018's universal-connectivity vision and BharatNet's still-incomplete village coverage.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB; Vajiram &amp; Ravi; Drishti IAS.</p>
</div>
