<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">1. National Agriculture Policy (NAP), 2000</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Announced in July 2000, in response to agriculture's sluggish growth through the 1990s — driven by inadequate capital, poor infrastructure, and market restrictions.</li>
<li>Seeks to actualize the untapped potential of Indian agriculture, targeting a growth rate above <b>4% per annum</b>.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Key Objectives &amp; Features</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Efficient use of natural resources — land, water, biodiversity.</li>
<li>Promote equity and inclusivity, uplifting small and marginal farmers.</li>
<li>Develop a demand-driven system responsive to both domestic and export markets, with WTO-compatible price protection.</li>
<li>Vision: make farming <b>competitive, sustainable, equitable, and remunerative</b> through modern technology and private investment.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Vajiram &amp; Ravi — "Agricultural Policy of India"; PIB.</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">1.1. Vehicle Schemes</h3>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi), 2019</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Provides <b>₹6,000/year</b> to landholding farmers in three instalments via Direct Benefit Transfer. Covers <b>11.8 crore farmers</b>, with over <b>₹2.80 lakh crore</b> disbursed to date.</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana), 2016</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Crop insurance combining farmers, insurers, banks, and government on one platform, providing risk cover against crop loss from natural disasters, pests, and disease. Now the <b>largest crop insurance scheme in the world</b> by farmer enrolment, and third-largest by premium volume.</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>PM-AASHA (Pradhan Mantri Annadata Aay Sanrakshan Abhiyan), 2018</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">An umbrella price-support scheme comprising the <b>Price Support Scheme (PSS)</b>, <b>Price Deficiency Payment Scheme (PDPS)</b>, and <b>Private Procurement &amp; Stockist Scheme (PPSS)</b> — states choose PSS or PDPS per oilseed crop each season; pulses/copra procured via PSS.</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF)</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;"><b>₹35,262 crore</b> sanctioned for 48,352 projects — including 11,165 warehouses, 10,307 primary processing units, 10,948 custom hiring centres, and 1,486 cold-storage projects.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)"; "10 Years of Transformation: Revolutionizing Agriculture"; Vajiram &amp; Ravi — "Agriculture Schemes in India".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">1.2. Key Challenges</h3>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #e6b0aa; border-left:5px solid #a93226; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Land Fragmentation:</b> small and marginal farmers (holding under 2 hectares) make up ~85% of all farmers, limiting economies of scale.</li>
<li><b>Climate Risk:</b> the Economic Survey 2017-18 estimated climate change could reduce average agricultural incomes by 15–18%, and up to 25% in unirrigated areas.</li>
<li><b>MSP Gaps:</b> harvest-time prices often fall below MSP due to procurement/logistics limitations, and MSP itself has risen only modestly (~5%/year, 2013-14 to 2023-24) — while legalising MSP remains contentious over inflation and export-competitiveness concerns.</li>
<li><b>Weak Infrastructure:</b> inadequate irrigation, storage, and transportation networks still cause post-harvest wastage and depress farmer returns despite AIF investment.</li>
<li><b>Unmeasured Income Goals:</b> progress toward the (separately announced) goal of doubling farmers' income lacks effective measurement, making it hard to assess whether NAP 2000's remunerative-farming vision is being met.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Vajiram &amp; Ravi — "Doubling Farmers' Income"; "Agricultural Distress in India"; Drishti IAS — "Legalising MSP in India".</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Way Forward</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Addressing land fragmentation through consolidation/leasing reform, building climate-resilient cropping systems, and closing the irrigation-storage-transport infrastructure gap remain central to converting PM-KISAN/PMFBY/PM-AASHA's reach into the higher, more stable farm incomes NAP 2000 envisioned.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Vajiram &amp; Ravi; Drishti IAS; PIB.</p>
</div>
