<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">1. National Policy for Empowerment of Women, 2001</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Adopted by the Government of India on <b>20 March 2001</b>, under the Ministry of Women and Child Development (WCD).</li>
<li>India's first formal, officially adopted national policy dedicated to women's empowerment.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Objectives</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Bring about the advancement, development, and empowerment of women.</li>
<li>Eliminate all forms of discrimination against women.</li>
<li>Strengthen processes that promote the all-round development of women through a coordinated approach to implementing schemes.</li>
<li>Create an enabling environment conducive to social change, favourable to women's full participation.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB.</p>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">2. Draft National Policy for Women, 2016/2017</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<p style="margin:0;">The National Policy for Women is an updated policy framework meant to replace the 2001 policy, incorporating emerging issues such as cyber safety, skill development, financial inclusion, and climate vulnerability, along with targeted support for widows, single women, migrant women, and women in conflict-affected regions.</p>
<p style="margin:10px 0 0 0;">A draft was released by the WCD Ministry in <b>May 2016</b> for public consultation. A revised draft — the <b>Draft National Policy for Women, 2017</b> — was prepared after incorporating stakeholder feedback, examined and approved by a <b>Group of Ministers (GoM)</b>, and submitted to the Cabinet for consideration.</p>
<p style="margin:10px 0 0 0; font-weight:600; color:#c0392b;">Status: Despite GoM approval, the policy has still not received final Cabinet approval and remains in draft/pending status.</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">2.1. Core Objectives</h3>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<p style="margin:0;">The draft aims to strengthen women's empowerment across three dimensions:</p>
<ul style="margin:8px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Legal &amp; Governance:</b> Enhanced legal protections, resource access, and gender-responsive administration.</li>
<li><b>Economic:</b> Skills development, technology adoption, and entrepreneurship expansion.</li>
<li><b>Social:</b> Quality healthcare, nutrition, reproductive rights, property rights, and access to financial services.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">2.3. Implementation Framework</h3>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Coordinated implementation across central ministries, state governments, and district authorities.</li>
<li>Partnerships with NGOs, the private sector, and academic institutions.</li>
<li>Aligns with and builds on existing schemes such as <b>Beti Bachao Beti Padhao</b> and <b>POSHAN Abhiyaan</b>.</li>
<li>Progress to be monitored using data from the <b>NCRB</b> (National Crime Records Bureau) and <b>NFHS</b> (National Family Health Survey).</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Vajiram &amp; Ravi — "National Policy for Women, Objectives, Priority Area, Implementation".</p>

<b>Governance Concern</b>
