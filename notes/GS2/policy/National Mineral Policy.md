<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">1. National Mineral Policy, 1993</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Coincided with early-1990s economic liberalization, opening the door to private investment in mineral exploration.</li>
<li>Sought to harmonize mining/mineral-sector policy with broader industrial and socio-economic development policy.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "National Mineral Policy".</p>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">2. National Mineral Policy, 2008</h2>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Aimed at a more transparent regulatory mechanism for mineral concessions, and strengthened roles for the Geological Survey of India and State Directorates of Mining.</li>
<li>Developed a Sustainable Development Framework covering indigenous-population stakeholder rights, and discouraged unscientific mining by promoting zero-waste mining.</li>
<li>Allowed private companies to apply for exploration permits under a <b>First-Come-First-Serve (FCFS)</b> system.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "National Mineral Policy, 2008".</p>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">3. National Mineral Policy (NMP), 2019 (Current)</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Replaced NMP 2008 in response to the MMDR Act amendments (2015, auction-based leases), Supreme Court sustainability rulings, geospatial/deep-sea exploration technology, and growing focus on tribal rights and community participation.</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Key Objectives &amp; Features</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Increase mineral exploration/resource mapping; promote sustainable, eco-friendly mining; reduce import dependence on strategic minerals.</li>
<li>Mining leases allocated through <b>open auctions</b> for transparency and fair competition.</li>
<li>Explore deep-sea mineral resources (polymetallic nodules) using advanced technology.</li>
<li>Promote mineral transport corridors and rail/port/waterway connectivity to cut logistics costs.</li>
<li>Use of the <b>District Mineral Fund (DMF)</b> for health, education, and livelihood of mining-affected communities.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Vajiram &amp; Ravi — "National Mineral Policy 2019, Objectives, Features, Implementation"; PIB.</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">3.1. Vehicle Schemes</h3>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>National Mineral Exploration Trust (NMET), 2015</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Established 14 August 2015 to boost regional and detailed mineral exploration.</p>
<ul style="margin:8px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Funded <b>270 exploration projects</b> — 162 completed, 108 ongoing; 11 completed projects auctioned by state governments.</li>
<li>25% incentive declared for deep-seated and critical-mineral exploration; ₹182.52 crore sanctioned to 19 States/GSI/IBM for equipment and tech upgrades.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "National Mineral Exploration Trust (NMET) Completes 162 Projects".</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>National Critical Mineral Mission (NCMM), 2025</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Approved with a <b>₹34,300 crore</b> outlay over seven years (2024-25 to 2030-31), to build a resilient critical-minerals value chain for green technologies, high-tech industry, and defence.</p>
<ul style="margin:8px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Geological Survey of India tasked with <b>1,200 exploration projects</b> over the mission period; 100+ critical-mineral blocks to be auctioned.</li>
<li>Centres of Excellence set up at major IITs/research labs; a <b>₹1,500 crore</b> incentive scheme approved for critical-mineral recycling capacity (2025-26 to 2030-31).</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "Cabinet Approves 'National Critical Mineral Mission'"; Vision IAS — "National Critical Mineral Mission (NCMM)".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">3.2. Key Challenges</h3>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #e6b0aa; border-left:5px solid #a93226; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Severe Import Dependence:</b> India has no working mining leases for cobalt, nickel, lithium, or neodymium; China controls ~60% of global rare-earth and critical-mineral production and ~80% of processing capacity.</li>
<li><b>High-Risk, Deep-Seated Deposits:</b> many critical minerals require high-risk investment in exploration and advanced extraction technology.</li>
<li><b>Skilled Workforce Gap:</b> the sector lacks workers capable of supporting complex extraction/processing; most new projects still depend on foreign consultants despite new IIT-based Centres of Excellence.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Drishti IAS — "Securing India's Critical Mineral Future"; PIB — "India's Critical Mineral Mission: Securing the Minerals of Tomorrow".</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Way Forward</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Scaling domestic exploration and processing capacity through NCMM, building the specialized workforce needed to reduce reliance on foreign consultants, and promoting secondary recovery from fly ash/tailings/red mud are central to reducing India's critical-mineral import dependence.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Drishti IAS; PIB; Vajiram &amp; Ravi.</p>
</div>
