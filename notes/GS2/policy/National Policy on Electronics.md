<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">1. National Policy on Electronics (NPE), 2012</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Its schemes/programmes successfully consolidated the foundations for a competitive Indian Electronics System Design and Manufacturing (ESDM) value chain, later built upon by NPE 2019.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "Cabinet approves the proposal of National Policy on Electronics 2019".</p>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">2. National Policy on Electronics (NPE), 2019 (Current)</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Replaces NPE 2012; envisions positioning India as a <b>global hub for ESDM</b>, developing core-component (including chipset) capabilities and an enabling environment for global competitiveness.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Key Targets &amp; Features</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Turnover target of <b>USD 400 billion</b> (~₹26 lakh crore) for the ESDM sector by 2025.</li>
<li>Production target of <b>1 billion mobile handsets</b> by 2025 (valued at USD 190 billion), including 600 million for export.</li>
<li>Incentives and support for manufacturing core electronic components; a special incentive package for mega-projects like semiconductor fabs and display fabrication.</li>
<li>Promotes a <b>trusted electronics value chain</b> to strengthen India's national cybersecurity profile.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "Cabinet approves the proposal of National Policy on Electronics 2019"; Drishti IAS — "National Policy on Electronics 2019".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">2.1. Vehicle Schemes</h3>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>PLI for Large Scale Electronics Manufacturing (LSEM), 2020</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Notified 1 April 2020, offering 4–6% incentive on incremental sales for target segments manufactured in India.</p>
<ul style="margin:8px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Cumulative investment: <b>₹12,390 crore</b>; cumulative production: <b>₹8,44,752 crore</b>; exports: <b>₹4,65,809 crore</b>; 1,30,330 direct jobs generated (till June 2025).</li>
<li>Mobile-phone production more than doubled — from ₹2.14 lakh crore (FY 2019-20) to ₹5.5 lakh crore (FY 2024-25); India shifted from net importer to <b>net exporter</b> of mobile phones, now the world's <b>2nd-largest mobile manufacturer</b>.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>SPECS (Scheme for Promotion of Manufacturing of Electronic Components and Semiconductors)</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Provides a <b>25% financial incentive on capital expenditure</b> for identified electronic goods, addressing domestic component/semiconductor manufacturing gaps.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "Mobile Manufacturing Sees Unprecedented Growth Under PLI"; "India emerges as Second Largest Mobile Manufacturing Country".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">2.2. Key Challenges</h3>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #e6b0aa; border-left:5px solid #a93226; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Severe Semiconductor Import Dependence:</b> India meets over 90% of its semiconductor needs from imports ($24 billion in 2022), and imports 80%+ of critical materials like silicon wafers and specialized chemicals.</li>
<li><b>High Fab Costs, Long Gestation:</b> semiconductor fabs take 4–5 years to become operational, with high setup/R&amp;D costs deterring investment given slow returns.</li>
<li><b>Low Value Addition:</b> India's electronics value addition sits around 15–20% (efforts underway to double it), and India contributes only ~3% to the global electronics manufacturing value chain.</li>
<li><b>Skilled Workforce Shortage:</b> ~220,000 semiconductor professionals currently employed, against a projected shortfall of 250,000–350,000 skilled workers by 2027.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Vajiram &amp; Ravi — "Semiconductor Industry in India"; "India's Electronics Manufacturing, Local Value Addition &amp; Cutting Imports"; Drishti IAS — "Building a Semiconductor Ecosystem in India".</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Way Forward</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Scaling domestic semiconductor fabrication capacity, raising value addition beyond assembly-stage manufacturing, and closing the projected skilled-workforce gap are central to converting PLI-LSEM's mobile-manufacturing success into NPE 2019's broader ESDM self-reliance goal.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Vajiram &amp; Ravi; Drishti IAS; PIB.</p>
</div>
