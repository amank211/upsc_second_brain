<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">1. National Water Policy, 1987</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>India's first National Water Policy, later reviewed and revised in 2002 and 2012.</li>
<li>Laid down an <b>Integrated Water Resources Management (IWRM)</b> approach, taking the river basin/sub-basin as the unit for planning, development, and management of water resources.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "Salient Features of The National Water Policy".</p>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">2. National Water Policy, 2002</h2>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Recognized water as a <b>prime natural resource, a basic human need, and a precious national asset</b>, with planning and management governed by a national perspective.</li>
<li>Committed to a well-developed information system for water data, via a network of data banks and databases at national/state level.</li>
<li>Aimed to bring water resources within the category of utilizable resources to the maximum extent possible.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "New National Water Policy".</p>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">3. National Water Policy, 2012 (Current)</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Assesses the existing situation and proposes a framework/plan of action with a <b>unified national perspective</b> on water conservation, development, and management.</li>
<li>Inter-basin transfers to be considered case-by-case, on merits, after evaluating environmental, economic, and social impacts.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Key Features</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Strong emphasis on <b>rainwater harvesting</b> — incentivizing States to revive traditional water-harvesting structures and encouraging harvesting in urban/industrial areas.</li>
<li>Preference for urban and rural domestic water supply from surface water, used conjunctively with groundwater and rainwater.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "National Water Policy"; "POLICY FOR RAINWATER HARVESTING".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">3.1. Vehicle Schemes</h3>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM), 2019</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Launched 15 August 2019 to provide tap water to every rural household by 2024; extended to <b>2028 (JJM 2.0)</b> with enhanced outlay.</p>
<ul style="margin:8px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Coverage rose from <b>3.23 crore households (16.71%)</b> at inception to over <b>15.72 crore households (81%+)</b> by October 2025.</li>
<li>11 States/UTs (Goa, Haryana, Telangana, Gujarat, Himachal Pradesh, Punjab, Mizoram, Arunachal Pradesh, etc.) have reached 100% rural tap-water coverage.</li>
<li>Estimated impact: 9 crore women freed from water-fetching duties; ~4,00,000 diarrhoeal deaths prevented; 14 million DALYs saved (WHO estimate).</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "Jal Jeevan Mission: Ensuring Tap Water for 15 Crore Rural..."; Vision IAS.</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Namami Gange Mission</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">River-rejuvenation vehicle for the Ganga basin — Uttarakhand's sewage-treatment capacity has quadrupled in six years, and over 130 drains flowing into the Ganga have been closed under the mission.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB.</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">3.2. Key Challenges</h3>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #e6b0aa; border-left:5px solid #a93226; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Groundwater Depletion:</b> overexploitation for irrigation is driving water tables to critical depths in agricultural states — in Punjab, levels have fallen from an ideal 50–60 feet to 150–200 feet in most places.</li>
<li><b>Widespread Water Stress:</b> per the 15th Finance Commission, roughly 600 million Indians faced high-to-extreme water stress in 2020.</li>
<li><b>Weak Community Implementation:</b> the Jal Shakti Abhiyan has struggled with insufficient funds and limited community participation, awareness, training, and ownership of local water-conservation projects.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Drishti IAS — "India's Groundwater Crisis"; "Addressing India's Looming Water Crisis"; "Preserving the Precious — Groundwater".</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Way Forward</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Strengthening community-led water-conservation efforts under Jal Shakti Abhiyan, scaling rainwater harvesting per NWP 2012's own emphasis, and regulating groundwater extraction in over-exploited states are central to easing India's water stress even as JJM and Namami Gange post strong delivery numbers.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Drishti IAS; PIB.</p>
</div>
