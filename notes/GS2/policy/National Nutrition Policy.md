<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">1. National Nutrition Policy (NNP), 1993</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Calls for a <b>multi-sectoral approach</b> — integrating agriculture, health, and food distribution — to achieve optimum nutrition for all.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Two-Track Core Strategy</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Short-Term Direct Interventions:</b> targeted nutrition support for children under 6, adolescent girls, and pregnant/lactating women; expanded safety nets; behaviour-change communication for mothers; food fortification; popularizing low-cost nutritious foods from local ingredients; controlling micronutrient deficiencies.</li>
<li><b>Long-Term Indirect Interventions:</b> using broader development-policy instruments to improve access and create the conditions for improved nutrition.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "National Nutrition Policy".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">1.1. Vehicle Schemes — Poshan Abhiyaan &amp; Mission Poshan 2.0</h3>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<p style="margin:0;"><b>Poshan Abhiyaan (2018)</b> — India's National Nutrition Mission, bringing multiple ministries/schemes under one framework with time-bound targets, digital monitoring, and a "Jan Andolan" (people's movement) approach.</p>
<p style="margin:10px 0 0 0;"><b>Mission Poshan 2.0 (2021)</b> — the current Integrated Nutrition Support Programme, covering <b>8.9 crore children (0–6 years)</b>, alongside adolescent girls, pregnant women, and lactating mothers.</p>
<p style="margin:10px 0 0 0;"><b>Achievements (NFHS-4 → NFHS-5):</b></p>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Stunting: 38.4% → 35.5%; Wasting: 21% → 19.3%; Underweight: 35.7% → 32.1%.</li>
<li>Infrastructure: 6,77,843 Anganwadi Centres with own buildings; 10,07,635 with functional toilets; 12,43,472 with drinking-water access.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "MISSION POSHAN 2.0"; Vajiram &amp; Ravi — "Poshan 2.0, Objectives, Components, Achievements, Challenges".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">1.2. Key Challenges — The Double Burden of Malnutrition</h3>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #e6b0aa; border-left:5px solid #a93226; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Persistent Undernutrition:</b> 36% of under-5 children remain stunted; India has the world's highest child wasting rate at 18.7% (over 21 million children); 57% of women aged 15–49 are anaemic.</li>
<li><b>Rising Obesity, Simultaneously:</b> overweight children rose from 2.7 million (2012) to 4.2 million (2024); adult obesity rose from 33.6 million to 71.4 million over a similar period — undernutrition and obesity now coexist.</li>
<li><b>Affordability Gap:</b> 40.4% of Indians cannot afford a healthy diet — many access calories through subsidized grains, but quality nutrition remains out of reach.</li>
<li><b>Urban Slum Paradox:</b> stunting and obesity coexist within the same households in slum settings, driven by poor-quality, energy-dense but nutrient-poor processed food.</li>
<li><b>Hard-to-Reach Pockets:</b> high stunting/wasting persists in tribal belts and urban slums despite national-level improvement.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Vajiram &amp; Ravi — "India's Double Burden of Malnutrition, Tackling Obesity"; Drishti IAS — "Rethinking India's Nutrition Strategy"; "India's Nutrition Problem".</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Way Forward</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">NNP 1993's original dual undernutrition focus now needs to expand to address the emerging obesity/NCD side of the double burden — through affordable healthy-diet access, targeted interventions in tribal/urban-slum pockets, and continued convergence under Mission Poshan 2.0.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Vajiram &amp; Ravi; Drishti IAS; PIB.</p>
</div>
