<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">1. National Population Policy (NPP), 2000</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Adopted in 2000 to address rapid population growth; primary objective is population stabilization through <b>voluntary and informed</b> family planning choice.</li>
<li>Reaffirms a <b>target-free approach</b> — no coercive numeric targets imposed on individuals or field workers — while simultaneously addressing contraception, maternal health, and child survival.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Three-Tier Goals</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Immediate:</b> address unmet needs for contraception, health-care infrastructure, and personnel; provide integrated basic reproductive and child health care.</li>
<li><b>Medium-term:</b> bring Total Fertility Rate (TFR) down to <b>replacement level (2.1)</b> by 2010.</li>
<li><b>Long-term:</b> achieve a <b>stable population by 2045</b>, at a level consistent with sustainable economic growth, social development, and environmental protection.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Other Key Features</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Guarantees free and compulsory education for every child up to 14 years, to reduce dropout rates and improve retention for both boys and girls.</li>
<li><b>National Socio-Demographic Goals</b> include 80% institutional deliveries and 100% deliveries attended by trained personnel.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Vajiram &amp; Ravi — "National Population Policy 2000, Objectives, Evolution, Features"; PIB.</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">1.1. Vehicle Scheme — Mission Parivar Vikas (2016)</h3>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<p style="margin:0;">Launched in 2016 to substantially increase access to family planning services in <b>146 high-fertility districts</b> (TFR ≥ 3.0) across seven high-focus states — Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, and Assam. Extended in November 2021 to the remaining districts of those states and all districts of six North-Eastern states.</p>
<p style="margin:10px 0 0 0;"><b>Key interventions:</b></p>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Clinical Outreach Teams (COT):</b> mobile teams delivering family-planning services in far-flung, underserved areas.</li>
<li><b>Antara Programme:</b> injectable contraceptive (MPA) — 23.41 lakh doses administered in MPV states, FY 2024-25 (up to Nov).</li>
<li>Awareness drives — Saas Bahu Sammelans, Nayi Pehel Kits, mobile publicity vans.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:10px 0 0 0;"><b>Recent performance (FY 2024-25, up to Nov):</b> 5.33 lakh sterilizations; 15 lakh Post-Partum IUCD (PPIUCD) insertions.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "Health Ministry to launch 'Mission Parivar Vikas'"; "Update on Family Planning &amp; Population Control in the country".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">1.2. Achievements</h3>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>India's <b>Total Fertility Rate reached 2.0</b> as per NFHS-5 (2019-21) — replacement level reached, roughly a decade after NPP 2000's 2010 target.</li>
<li><b>31 of 36</b> States/UTs have achieved replacement-level fertility (NFHS-5).</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "India has achieved a Total Fertility Rate of 2.0".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">1.3. Key Challenges</h3>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #e6b0aa; border-left:5px solid #a93226; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Sharp Regional Disparity:</b> Bihar and Uttar Pradesh still record birth rates of 20.8–26.2 per 1,000, far above the southern states; nearly half of India's projected population growth to 2036 will come from northern states, versus just 9% from the south.</li>
<li><b>Skewed Sex Ratio at Birth:</b> stands at 931 (NFHS-5), reflecting continued son preference — population stabilization is not just about numbers but also gender parity, which NPP 2000 under-addressed.</li>
<li><b>Delimitation Controversy:</b> southern states (Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu) that succeeded at fertility reduction now fear losing parliamentary seats in a population-based delimitation exercise — a perverse political disincentive against the policy's own success.</li>
<li><b>Missed Timeline:</b> the medium-term replacement-fertility target (2010) was met only around a decade late (NFHS-5, 2019-21), showing how uneven implementation across states delayed the national aggregate.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Vajiram &amp; Ravi — "Tackling Delimitation by Reversing Population Control"; Drishti IAS — "Population Stabilization"; "Rethinking New Population Strategy".</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Way Forward</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Sustaining Mission Parivar Vikas in the remaining high-fertility pockets, addressing skewed sex ratios through targeted gender-parity interventions, and resolving the delimitation-linked disincentive facing successful states are key to converting national TFR success into balanced, equitable demographic outcomes.</p>
