<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">1. National Environment Policy (NEP), 2006</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>India's response to its national commitment to a clean environment — synergizes environmental and economic policy with appropriate institutional mechanisms.</li>
<li>Integrates the three pillars of sustainable development — <b>economic development, social development, and environmental protection</b> — treating environmental protection as an essential component of progress, not an obstacle to it.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Key Principles</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Conserve critical environmental resources while ensuring equitable access for poorer communities.</li>
<li><b>Polluter Pays Principle</b> — those responsible for pollution bear the cost of prevention/remediation.</li>
<li><b>Precautionary Principle</b> — lack of full scientific certainty is not a reason to delay measures against serious or irreversible environmental damage.</li>
<li>Efficient resource use and application of good-governance principles to environmental management.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change"; Vision IAS — "Environment Impact Assessment"; Drishti IAS — "National Green Tribunal (NGT)".</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<p style="margin:0;"><b>Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA):</b> operationalized via the EIA Notification, 2006 (under the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986), requiring a two-stage screening-and-detailed-assessment process and prior Environmental Clearance before major industrial/construction projects can begin.</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">1.1. Vehicle — National Action Plan on Climate Change (NAPCC), 2008</h3>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<p style="margin:0;">Launched in 2008 to operationalize NEP 2006's sustainable-development goals through <b>eight National Missions</b>:</p>
<ol style="margin:8px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>National Solar Mission</li>
<li>National Mission for Enhanced Energy Efficiency</li>
<li>National Mission on Sustainable Habitat</li>
<li>National Water Mission</li>
<li>National Mission for Sustaining the Himalayan Eco-system</li>
<li>National Mission for a Green India</li>
<li>National Mission for Sustainable Agriculture</li>
<li>National Mission on Strategic Knowledge for Climate Change</li>
</ol>
<p style="margin:10px 0 0 0;"><b>Achievements:</b> grid-connected solar capacity rose from 25 MW (2010-11) to ~36.32 GW (Oct 2020), against a 175 GW renewable-energy-by-2022 goal; vehicle-emission norms leapfrogged from Bharat Stage-IV directly to Bharat Stage-VI by April 2020, alongside EV promotion.</p>
<p style="margin:10px 0 0 0;"><b>State Action Plans on Climate Change (SAPCC):</b> 34 States/UTs have prepared (and some updated) SAPCCs aligned with NAPCC.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "Progress in Eight Missions on Climate Change"; Vajiram &amp; Ravi — "National Action Plan on Climate Change (NAPCC), Features, Challenges".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">1.2. Key Challenges</h3>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #e6b0aa; border-left:5px solid #a93226; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Vague, Unfunded State Plans:</b> SAPCCs often lack clear, specific actions that facilitate implementation, and many states assumed funding would come from the Centre or external sources rather than budgeting for it themselves.</li>
<li><b>Top-Down Design:</b> SAPCC's top-down formulation has left many states with weak ownership, leadership, and political will to execute their own plans.</li>
<li><b>Institutional Gaps:</b> lack of dedicated nodal officers within departments to drive climate action has slowed cross-sectoral coordination.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Vajiram &amp; Ravi — "National Action Plan on Climate Change (NAPCC), Features, Challenges"; Vision IAS — "Delhi government to overhaul State Action Plan on Climate Change (SAPCC)".</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Way Forward</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Nominating climate nodal officers in each key department, developing detailed and regularly updated project reports at the state level, and tapping international climate finance for adaptation costs are key to closing the gap between NAPCC's eight missions and uneven state-level execution.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Vajiram &amp; Ravi; Vision IAS; PIB.</p>
</div>
