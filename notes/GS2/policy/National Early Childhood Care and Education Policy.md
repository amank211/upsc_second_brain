<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">1. National Early Childhood Care and Education (ECCE) Policy, 2013</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Notified in the Gazette on <b>12 October 2013</b> — recognizes ECCE as an indispensable foundation for lifelong development/learning and the most cost-effective way to break the intergenerational cycle of disadvantage.</li>
<li>Addresses <b>158.7 million</b> children under six years (Census 2011).</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Key Features</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Encompasses <b>care, health, nutrition, play, and early learning</b> within a protective, enabling environment.</li>
<li>States/UTs made responsible for rolling out their own State/UT ECCE Policies and Action Plans within three years of notification.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "National Early Childhood Care and Education Policy".</p>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">2. Integration into NEP 2020's Foundational Stage</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>NEP 2020's <b>5+3+3+4</b> structure formally brings the <b>3–6 age group</b> into the school continuum for the first time — 3 years of Balvatika plus Grades 1–2 form the "Foundational Stage".</li>
<li>Complemented by the <b>National Curriculum Framework for Foundational Stage (NCF-FS), 2022</b>, and <b>Navchetana</b> — the National Framework for Early Childhood Stimulation (birth–3 years), released 2024, alongside a National Curriculum for ECCE (3–6 years).</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "Ministry of Women and Child Development is launching a National Curriculum for Early Childhood Care and Education"; Vision IAS — "Navchetana - National Framework for Early Childhood Stimulation (ECS) released".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">2.1. Vehicle — NIPUN Bharat, 2021</h3>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<p style="margin:0;">Launched 5 July 2021 under the Samagra Shiksha umbrella scheme — a five-tier (National-State-District-Block-School) mission to ensure every child attains <b>Foundational Literacy and Numeracy (FLN)</b> by the end of Grade 3, targeted for 2026-27.</p>
<p style="margin:10px 0 0 0;"><b>Achievements:</b></p>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>The 2024 Rashtriya Sarvekshan covered 21.15 lakh students and 2.7 lakh teachers.</li>
<li>NISHTHA FLN+ECCE training completed by 15.57 lakh+ users.</li>
<li>Recent survey: 64% average language proficiency, 60% average math proficiency among assessed students — progress, but still short of universal goals.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "NIPUN Bharat Mission and Foundational Literacy and Numeracy"; "Union Education Minister launches NIPUN Bharat Programme".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">2.2. Key Challenges</h3>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #e6b0aa; border-left:5px solid #a93226; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>ASER 2024 Learning Gaps:</b> 48.7% of Class 5 students can read fluently, but only 30.7% can solve a basic division problem — an 18-percentage-point gap between literacy and numeracy; more than half of Grade 3 students cannot read Grade-2-level text or do simple subtraction.</li>
<li><b>Persistent Illiteracy in Older Youth:</b> roughly 25% of youth aged 14–18 still cannot read a standard Class II text fluently in their regional language.</li>
<li><b>Systemic Causes:</b> weak accountability, poor pedagogy, rote learning, and automated grade promotion have inflated academic progression while hollowing out actual foundational skills.</li>
<li><b>Anganwadi-School Integration Gap:</b> the anganwadi system — especially its education component — remains under-funded and poorly integrated with the formal school system, weakening the ECCE-to-Foundational-Stage handoff.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Drishti IAS — "India's Foundational Literacy and Numeracy Crisis"; "ASER 2024 and Elementary Education"; "Overhauling Early Childhood Education".</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Way Forward</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Better anganwadi-school integration, professionalizing the ECCE workforce, and shifting from rote/automated-promotion practices toward genuine outcome accountability are central to closing the ASER-documented gap between NIPUN Bharat's 2026-27 FLN target and current classroom reality.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Drishti IAS; PIB; Vision IAS.</p>
</div>
