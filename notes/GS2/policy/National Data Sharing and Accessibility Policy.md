<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">1. National Data Sharing and Accessibility Policy (NDSAP), 2012</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Facilitates access to Government of India-owned <b>shareable data</b>, in both human-readable and machine-readable form, through a nationwide network, on a proactive and periodically-updatable basis.</li>
<li>Fosters accessibility and easier sharing of <b>non-personal data</b> for scientific, economic, and social developmental purposes.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Key Features</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Scope limited to India-generated data owned by the <b>Central Government</b> that is designated shareable.</li>
<li>Establishes a data-governance framework to make government-held information more accessible to researchers, innovators, and the public.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "National Data Sharing and Accessibility Policy – 2012 approved".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">1.1. Vehicle Schemes</h3>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Open Government Data (OGD) Platform / data.gov.in:</b> the primary public-facing portal operationalizing NDSAP's proactive-disclosure mandate.</li>
<li><b>National Data and Analytics Platform (NDAP), NITI Aayog:</b> lets users search, merge, visualize, and download standardized government datasets for cross-sectoral analysis — hosting <b>885 datasets across 15 sectors and 46 Ministries</b> (as of Feb 2023).</li>
<li><b>Compendium of Datasets and Registries in India, 2024</b> (MoSPI): consolidated metadata for ~270 datasets/registries from 40 Ministries/Departments, spanning agriculture, health, education, labour, and more.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "NITI Aayog Launches the National Data &amp; Analytics Platform"; "NSO India releases the 'Compendium of Datasets and Registries in India, 2024'".</p>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">2. Proposed Successor — Draft National Data Governance Framework Policy, 2022</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<p style="margin:0;">Aims to ensure non-personal and anonymized data — from both government and private entities — is safely accessible to the research and innovation ecosystem, via an institutional framework of data/metadata rules, standards, and protocols balancing sharing against privacy, security, and trust.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "National Data Governance Framework Policy"; Drishti IAS — "Draft National Data Governance Framework Policy".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">2.1. Key Challenges</h3>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #e6b0aa; border-left:5px solid #a93226; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Anonymization Isn't Enough:</b> the draft's reliance on anonymization standards is not a sufficient privacy safeguard — downstream processing and automated re-identification tools can still expose group privacy even in anonymized citizen datasets.</li>
<li><b>No Dedicated Non-Personal-Data Law:</b> while personal data is now covered by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, there is still no equivalent accountability/remedy framework for non-personal data misuse — coercive collection or breaches involving non-personal datasets remain largely unaddressed.</li>
<li><b>Weak Enforceability:</b> the current governance framework (including the draft policy itself) lacks enforceability and operational clarity, leaving most non-personal data effectively unregulated.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Drishti IAS — "Recalibrating Non Personal Data"; "Data Governance in India"; "A Social Contract for Data".</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Way Forward</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Finalizing the National Data Governance Framework Policy with enforceable rules — and closing the non-personal-data accountability gap the DPDP Act, 2023 leaves open — is central to letting NDAP/OGD's growing dataset base be used safely for research and innovation.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Drishti IAS; PIB.</p>
</div>
