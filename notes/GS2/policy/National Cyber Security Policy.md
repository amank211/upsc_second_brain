<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">1. National Cyber Security Policy (NCSP), 2013</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Vision &amp; Mission</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Vision: a secure and resilient cyberspace for citizens, businesses, and government.</li>
<li>Mission: protect information and information infrastructure, build capabilities to prevent/respond to cyber threats, reduce vulnerabilities, and minimize damage from incidents — via institutional structures, people, process, technology, and cooperation.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Key Features</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Creates a secure cyber ecosystem, reduces vulnerabilities, and enhances trust in electronic transactions.</li>
<li>Safeguards personal, financial/banking, and sovereign data through frameworks, legal structures, and public-private partnerships.</li>
<li>Focuses on protecting critical infrastructure and promoting cybersecurity practices nationwide.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Vajiram &amp; Ravi — "National Cyber Security Policy 2013, Objectives, Need, Features".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">1.1. Vehicle Institutions</h3>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>CERT-In (Indian Computer Emergency Response Team)</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Operates an automated cyber-threat exchange platform, proactively collecting, analysing, and sharing tailored alerts across sectors; runs the <b>National Cyber Coordination Centre (NCCC)</b> for situational awareness.</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C), 2018</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">A Ministry of Home Affairs scheme (a "7-pronged" scheme) building a framework for prevention, detection, investigation, and prosecution of cybercrime.</p>
<ul style="margin:8px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>National Cybercrime Reporting Portal (NCRP)</b>, launched 2019 — a citizen-centric complaint platform.</li>
<li><b>Samanvaya Platform</b>: an MIS/data-repository/coordination platform for Law Enforcement Agencies, giving analytics-based interstate crime/criminal linkages.</li>
<li><b>Achievements (to 31 Oct 2025):</b> ₹7,130+ crore saved across 23.02 lakh complaints; 16,840 accused arrested; 1,05,129 cyber-investigation assistance requests handled.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "INDIAN CYBER CRIME COORDINATION CENTRE (I4C)"; "CYBER SECURITY AND FINANCIAL FRAUD COMBAT".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">1.2. Key Challenges</h3>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #e6b0aa; border-left:5px solid #a93226; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Policy Predates Today's Threat Scale:</b> NCSP 2013 was framed well before India's current internet base (900 million+ users expected by 2025) and today's threat landscape — analysts argue it needs a more comprehensive replacement.</li>
<li><b>Rising Ransomware:</b> over 75% of Indian organizations have faced ransomware attacks, each breach costing an average of ₹35 crore in damage.</li>
<li><b>High-Profile Data Breaches:</b> the Juspay breach exposed 35 million customers' data; the 2022 Air India cyberattack compromised ~4.5 million customer records including passport and partial credit-card details.</li>
<li><b>Professional Shortage:</b> acute scarcity of cybersecurity professionals, cyber-forensics facilities, and standardized coordination among stakeholders.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Drishti IAS — "Rethinking India's Cyber Defence"; "India's Cybersecurity Challenge: Threats and Strategies".</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Way Forward</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Finalizing a comprehensive National Cyber Security Strategy to replace the decade-plus-old 2013 policy, expanding cybersecurity workforce capacity, and strengthening CERT-In/I4C coordination remain central to closing the gap between India's digital growth and its cyber-defence readiness.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Drishti IAS; Vajiram &amp; Ravi; PIB.</p>
</div>
