<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">1. National Policy on Urban Street Vendors (NPUSV), 2004 / 2009</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>The 2004 policy was revised into the National Policy on Urban Street Vendors, 2009, recognizing street vendors' significant contribution to urban society.</li>
<li>Aims to enable vendors to earn a decent livelihood through conditions for decent work, without obstructing the public, reflecting the Constitution's guarantee of equal protection and the right to practice any profession or business.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Key Features</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Aimed at protection of livelihood rights and provision of social security to urban street vendors.</li>
<li>Underscored the need for a dedicated <b>legislative framework</b> to let vendors pursue an honest living without harassment — the gap that the 2014 Act would eventually fill.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "National Policy on Urban Street Vendors".</p>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">2. Street Vendors (Protection of Livelihood and Regulation of Street Vending) Act, 2014</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<p style="margin:0;">Enacted to legalize vendors' vending rights and protect/regulate street vending in cities, with State-level rules/schemes executed by Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) through by-laws, planning, and regulation — converting NPUSV's policy commitments into binding law.</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Key Provisions</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Commits to accommodating all <b>"existing" vendors</b> in designated vending zones and issuing them <b>Vending Certificates (CoV)</b>.</li>
<li>Establishes participatory <b>Town Vending Committees (TVCs)</b> — street-vendor representatives must form 40% of TVC members, with a 33% sub-quota for women vendors.</li>
<li>Aims to create a fair, transparent environment for vendors to operate free of harassment and eviction fear.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Vision IAS — "Street Vendors (Protection of Livelihood and Regulation of Street Vending) Act, 2014"; Vajiram &amp; Ravi.</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">2.1. Vehicle Scheme — PM SVANidhi</h3>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<p style="margin:0;">"PM Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi" — launched 2020, providing collateral-free working-capital loans in three tranches (up to ₹15,000 → ₹25,000 → ₹50,000).</p>
<p style="margin:10px 0 0 0;"><b>Achievements:</b></p>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Over <b>1.12 crore loans</b> worth <b>₹17,800+ crore</b> disbursed to ~1 crore beneficiaries, including 34.81 lakh women; incomes boosted by an estimated <b>20%</b>.</li>
<li>~47 lakh digitally active beneficiaries have made 557+ crore digital transactions worth ₹6.09 lakh crore, earning ₹241 crore in cashback.</li>
<li><b>SVANidhi se Samriddhi:</b> 46 lakh beneficiaries profiled across 3,564 ULBs, leading to 1.38 crore other welfare-scheme sanctions.</li>
<li>Won the Prime Minister's Award for Excellence in Public Administration (2023) for Innovation.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "PM SVANidhi: From Survival to Self-Reliance"; Vision IAS — "Six years of PM SVANidhi".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">2.2. Key Challenges</h3>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #e6b0aa; border-left:5px solid #a93226; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Continued Harassment/Eviction:</b> despite the Act, vendors still face harassment and eviction, often driven by bureaucratic attitudes that treat them as illegal occupants.</li>
<li><b>TVCs Not Vendor-Led:</b> Town Vending Committees are often controlled by city authorities rather than vendors themselves, with women's representation frequently only tokenistic; TVCs remain unconstituted in several states, leaving vendors exposed to eviction.</li>
<li><b>Weak Grievance Redress:</b> only nine states (including Assam, Kerala, Punjab) have constituted the mandated Grievance Redressal Committees (GRCs).</li>
<li><b>Incomplete Registration:</b> identity cards and Certificates of Vending have not been issued to all eligible vendors; only ~31% of towns in states with a notified scheme have framed formal street-vending plans.</li>
<li><b>Restrictive Ceiling:</b> the Act's 2.5%-of-population ceiling for accommodating vendors in an area often falls well short of the actual number of vendors operating there.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Vision IAS — "A decade of Street Vendors Act, 2014"; Drishti IAS — "Street Vendors: Assessing their Significance and Struggles"; "Challenges Faced by Street Vendors".</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Way Forward</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Ensuring genuinely vendor-led TVCs, universal issuance of vending certificates, functioning GRCs in every state, and a realistic vendor-accommodation ceiling are key to converting the Act's legal protections and PM SVANidhi's credit access into lived security for street vendors.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Vision IAS; Drishti IAS; PIB.</p>
</div>
