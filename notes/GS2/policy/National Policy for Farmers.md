<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">1. National Commission on Farmers (Swaminathan Commission), 2004</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Constituted in 2004 under Dr. M.S. Swaminathan, tasked with enhancing productivity, profitability, and sustainability of major farming systems; attracting/retaining educated youth in farming; and a medium-term food/nutrition security strategy.</li>
<li><b>Key recommendation:</b> the <b>C2+50% formula</b> for MSP — setting MSP at least 50% above the comprehensive cost of production (C2, including imputed capital cost and land rent) to guarantee farmers a 50% return.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "National Policy for Farmers, 2007"; Drishti IAS — "MS Swaminathan".</p>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">2. National Policy for Farmers (NPF), 2007</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<p style="margin:0;">Intended to rejuvenate the farm sector and bring lasting improvement in farmers' economic condition, drawing on the Swaminathan Commission's broader findings.</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Key Provisions</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Asset reforms</b> in land, water, livestock, fisheries, and bio-resources.</li>
<li>Supply of good quality seeds/disease-free planting material; <b>soil health passbooks</b> and integrated pest management.</li>
<li>Region- and crop-specific implements/machinery; support services for women farmers.</li>
<li>Timely, adequate institutional credit at reasonable rates, and farmer-friendly insurance instruments.</li>
<li>ICT-enabled agricultural extension via <b>farm schools</b>; an agricultural bio-security system.</li>
<li>Coverage of farmers under a comprehensive <b>national social security scheme</b>.</li>
<li>Effective MSP implementation nationwide, community foodgrain banks, and stronger agricultural market infrastructure/terminal markets.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "National Policy for Farmers, 2007".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">2.1. Vehicle Schemes</h3>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Soil Health Card Scheme:</b> operationalizes NPF's "soil health passbook" provision — institutionalizes scientific, soil-specific nutrient recommendations. ~26 crore cards issued by March 2026.</li>
<li><b>e-NAM (National Agriculture Market):</b> the market-infrastructure vehicle — 1,410 mandis integrated across 23 States/4 UTs; 1.78 crore farmers and 2.63 lakh traders registered; trade worth ~₹4.01 lakh crore recorded (as of Dec 2024).</li>
<li><b>PM Kisan Maandhan Yojana (PM-KMY), 2019:</b> the "comprehensive social security scheme" vehicle — ₹3,000/month pension for small/marginal farmers after age 60; ~24.95 lakh farmers enrolled (Feb 2026).</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "Five Successful Years of PM-KMY"; "Brief on the achievements through schemes and programmes of Ministry of Agriculture &amp; Farmers Welfare"; Vajiram &amp; Ravi — "Soil Health Card Scheme".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">2.2. Key Challenges</h3>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #e6b0aa; border-left:5px solid #a93226; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>C2+50% Never Adopted:</b> the Swaminathan Commission's core MSP recommendation was not incorporated into NPF 2007, and remains unimplemented today over fiscal and market-distortion concerns.</li>
<li><b>A2+FL vs. C2 Gap:</b> MSP is still computed on the narrower A2+FL formula (paid-out costs only); during 2019-20, the gap between CACP-recommended MSP and the Swaminathan C2+50% price was 45.92% for jowar and 42.40% for ragi.</li>
<li><b>Ongoing Farmer Protests:</b> the 2020–24 farmer protests centred substantially on demands to legally guarantee MSP on the Swaminathan formula — a demand still unresolved.</li>
<li><b>Fiscal Concerns:</b> government reluctance to legalize MSP stems from worries about inflationary pressure and reduced export competitiveness of Indian agricultural produce.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Drishti IAS — "Law on MSP: Farmers Back on Road"; "Legalising MSP in India: Challenges and Way Forward"; "Farmers Protest 2.0 and MSP".</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Way Forward</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Resolving the MSP-legalization debate — balancing farmer income security against fiscal and trade-competitiveness concerns — remains the central unfinished item from NPF 2007's own Swaminathan Commission origins, even as vehicle schemes like e-NAM and PM-KMY have expanded market access and social security.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Drishti IAS; PIB.</p>
</div>
