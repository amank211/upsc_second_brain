<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">1. National Policy on Information Technology (NPIT), 2012</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Approved by Cabinet to leverage Information &amp; Communication Technology (ICT) to address India's economic and developmental challenges.</li>
<li>Targets: grow the IT market to <b>USD 300 billion</b> and create <b>10 million additional jobs</b> by 2020.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Key Objectives</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Encourage ICT adoption in key economic/strategic sectors to boost competitiveness and productivity; fiscal benefits for SMEs and startups adopting IT.</li>
<li>Create a pool of <b>10 million additional skilled ICT manpower</b>; make at least one person in every household <b>e-literate</b>.</li>
<li>Mandatory delivery of, and affordable access to, all public services in electronic mode.</li>
<li>Enhance transparency, accountability, efficiency, reliability, and decentralization in government service delivery.</li>
<li>Leverage ICT for social-sector goals — education, health, rural development, financial services.</li>
<li>Make India a global hub for language technologies, with content accessible in all Indian languages to bridge the digital divide.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "Cabinet Approves National Policy on Information Technology 2012".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">1.1. Vehicle — Digital India (2015)</h3>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<p style="margin:0;">Built around <b>9 pillars</b> — broadband highways, mobile connectivity, public internet access, e-governance, e-education, e-healthcare, financial inclusion, digital agriculture, and emerging tech (AI, blockchain) — to operationalize NPIT 2012's ICT vision.</p>
<p style="margin:10px 0 0 0;"><b>Achievements:</b></p>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>~2.15 lakh Gram Panchayats (~97%) connected under BharatNet (Jan 2026); ~7 lakh km of optical fibre cable laid.</li>
<li><b>UPI</b>: 376 banks onboarded, 730 crore transactions worth ₹11.9 lakh crore processed (monthly figures cited).</li>
<li>Electronics production rose from <b>₹1.9 lakh crore (FY 2014-15)</b> to <b>~₹12 lakh crore (March 2026)</b>; India is now the world's second-largest mobile-phone manufacturer.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "The Digital India 11 Years of Transformation"; Vajiram &amp; Ravi — "Digital India Mission, About, Approach, Methodology, Achievements".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">1.2. Key Challenges</h3>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #e6b0aa; border-left:5px solid #a93226; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Rural-Urban Digital Divide:</b> only ~24% of rural households have internet access versus ~66% in urban areas; rural internet penetration stood at just 37% as of 2023.</li>
<li><b>Low Digital Literacy:</b> India's digital literacy rate is estimated at ~37% (NASSCOM) — well short of NPIT 2012's "one e-literate person per household" goal.</li>
<li><b>Infrastructure Bottlenecks:</b> unreliable electricity, affordability of devices/connectivity, topographical difficulty, and backhaul network gaps continue to slow last-mile rural connectivity.</li>
<li><b>Rising Cybersecurity Risk:</b> 13.91 lakh cybersecurity incidents recorded in 2022 alone, against an estimated shortage of 8 lakh cybersecurity professionals — a widening gap between digital adoption and defensive capacity, even as the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 has since been enacted.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Vajiram &amp; Ravi — "Digital Divide, Aspects, Causes, Impact, Reforms and Initiatives"; Drishti IAS — "Navigating India's Digital Growth"; "10 Years of Digital India Mission".</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Way Forward</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Closing the rural-urban connectivity and digital-literacy gaps, and scaling cybersecurity workforce and infrastructure alongside adoption, are central to converting Digital India's headline achievements (UPI, electronics manufacturing) into the inclusive digital economy NPIT 2012 envisioned.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Vajiram &amp; Ravi; Drishti IAS; PIB.</p>
</div>
