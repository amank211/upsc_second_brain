<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">1. Draft Science, Technology and Innovation Policy (STIP), 2020</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Jointly initiated by the Office of the Principal Scientific Adviser and the Department of Science and Technology — India's <b>5th</b> STIP, drafted at the height of the COVID-19 pandemic.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Key Features</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Democratic Access to Knowledge:</b> envisions free access to all journals (Indian and foreign) for every Indian, funded via a centrally-negotiated payment mechanism.</li>
<li><b>Gender Parity:</b> women's participation in Indian R&amp;D has doubled over six years but still stands at only ~16% overall.</li>
<li><b>Traditional Knowledge Integration:</b> proposes institutional architecture to bring Traditional Knowledge Systems and grassroots innovation into mainstream education, research, and innovation.</li>
<li><b>National STI Observatory:</b> a proposed central repository for all STI-ecosystem data.</li>
<li><b>STI Development Bank:</b> a proposed corpus fund for long-term investment in select strategic research areas.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Drishti IAS — "Science Technology and Innovation Policy"; "Draft National Science, Technology and Innovation Policy".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">1.1. Vehicle — Anusandhan National Research Foundation (ANRF)</h3>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<p style="margin:0;">Established via the <b>ANRF Act, 2023</b> (in force 5 February 2024), replacing the Science and Engineering Research Board (SERB) — to seed, grow, and promote R&amp;D and a research/innovation culture across universities, colleges, and research institutions.</p>
<ul style="margin:8px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Targets mobilizing <b>₹50,000 crore over 2023–28</b> via the ANRF Fund, Innovation Fund, Science and Engineering Research Fund, and Special Purpose Funds (28% public, 72% private/philanthropic); ₹2,000 crore allocated for BE 2024-25.</li>
<li><b>MAHA (Mission for Advancement in High-impact Areas)</b> initiative — e.g. <b>MAHA-EV</b>, focused on Tropical EV batteries/cells, power electronics, and EV charging infrastructure, with mandatory 10% industry cost contribution.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Vajiram &amp; Ravi — "Anusandhan National Research Foundation, Core Objectives, Latest News"; Vision IAS — "ANRF Launches PMECRG and MAHA-EV initiative".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">1.2. Key Challenges</h3>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #e6b0aa; border-left:5px solid #a93226; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Persistently Low GERD:</b> India's Gross Expenditure on R&amp;D remains around <b>0.64–0.7% of GDP</b>, far below China (~2.4%), the USA (~3.5%), and South Korea (~4.9%).</li>
<li><b>Weak Private-Sector Participation:</b> private industry contributes only ~37% of India's total R&amp;D spend, versus 70–80% in leading innovation economies — leaving R&amp;D disproportionately government-funded.</li>
<li><b>Academia-Industry Silos:</b> research institutions and industry largely operate separately, limiting interdisciplinary and applied innovation.</li>
<li><b>Thin Researcher Base:</b> only ~255 researchers per million population, well below major developed and emerging economies.</li>
<li><b>Global Ranking Gap:</b> India ranks 39th on the Global Innovation Index 2024 — far behind China (11th) — despite a large STEM talent pool, reflecting the disconnect between human capital and R&amp;D funding.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Vajiram &amp; Ravi — "Low R&amp;D Investment in India, Causes, Challenges, Initiatives"; Drishti IAS — "Reviving India's R&amp;D Funding"; "Reforming India's R&amp;D Ecosystem".</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Way Forward</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Mobilizing ANRF's targeted private/philanthropic share of funding, deepening academia-industry linkages, and expanding the researcher base are central to closing the gap between STIP's ambitions and India's still-low R&amp;D-to-GDP ratio.</p>
