<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">1. National Policy on Resettlement and Rehabilitation for Project Affected Families, 2003</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>India's first national policy dedicated to the resettlement and rehabilitation of families displaced by development projects.</li>
<li>Provided the initial framework later replaced by the more detailed NPRR 2007.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "New Resettlement and Rehabilitation Policy".</p>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">2. National Policy on Rehabilitation and Resettlement, 2007</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Approved to replace NPRR 2003, aiming to minimize displacement and promote non-displacing or least-displacing alternatives wherever possible.</li>
<li>Seeks to balance the need for land for development against protecting the interests of landowners, tenants, the landless, agricultural/non-agricultural labourers, artisans, and others dependent on the affected land.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Key Features</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Covers all cases of involuntary displacement; mandates a <b>Social Impact Assessment (SIA)</b> where displacement affects 400+ families in plain areas or 200+ in tribal/hilly/Scheduled areas.</li>
<li>Made consultation with Gram Sabhas or public hearings compulsory, and established the principle of <b>rehabilitation before displacement</b>.</li>
<li>Provided a monthly pension for vulnerable affected persons — the disabled, destitute, orphans, widows, and unmarried girls.</li>
<li>Each Scheduled Tribe (and then Scheduled Caste) affected family to be given land-for-land allotment where Government land is available in the resettlement area.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB — "New Resettlement and Rehabilitation Policy".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">2.1. Vehicle — National Rehabilitation Commission</h3>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<p style="margin:0;">Set up by the Central Government under NPRR 2007, empowered to exercise <b>independent oversight</b> over the rehabilitation and resettlement of project-affected families — the policy's dedicated monitoring vehicle.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB.</p>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">3. RFCTLARR Act, 2013 — From Policy to Statute</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<p style="margin:0;">The <b>Right to Fair Compensation and Transparency in Land Acquisition, Rehabilitation and Resettlement (RFCTLARR) Act, 2013</b> converted NPRR 2007's non-binding policy commitments into binding law — providing fair compensation, transparency in land acquisition, and mandatory rehabilitation for affected families. It remains India's current legal vehicle for R&amp;R.</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#f5e9dc; border:1px solid #e0c6a3; border-left:5px solid #c0392b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Key Provisions</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Compensation:</b> up to <b>4 times</b> the market value in rural areas and <b>2 times</b> in urban areas.</li>
<li><b>R&amp;R as integral to acquisition:</b> Chapters V and VI mandate rehabilitation and resettlement (housing, employment, allowances) as part of the land acquisition plan itself, not an afterthought.</li>
<li><b>Consent requirements:</b> 80% landowner consent for private projects; 70% for PPP projects.</li>
<li><b>Scheduled Areas protection (Section 41(1)):</b> as far as possible, no acquisition in Scheduled Areas; where unavoidable, only as a demonstrable last resort.</li>
<li><b>Return of unused land:</b> land unused for 5 years must be returned to the original owners or the land bank.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Vision IAS — "RFCTLARR Act, 2013"; Vajiram &amp; Ravi — "Land Acquisition Act 2013".</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">3.1. Vehicle — National Monitoring Committee</h3>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<p style="margin:0;">A <b>National Monitoring Committee (NMC)</b> has been set up to look into issues of displacement, compensation, payment, and resettlement — including for tribal persons — succeeding the 2007 policy's National Rehabilitation Commission as the Act's oversight vehicle.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: PIB.</p>
</div>

<h3 style="background-color:#4a6b7c; color:#ffffff; padding:8px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:24px;">3.2. Key Challenges</h3>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #e6b0aa; border-left:5px solid #a93226; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Assessments Reduced to Formality:</b> Social and Environmental Impact Assessments are sometimes carried out as box-ticking exercises, with reports pre-designed to favour acquisition rather than genuinely evaluate impact.</li>
<li><b>Weak Monitoring:</b> the National Monitoring Committee has failed to quell discontent in large projects such as the Ken-Betwa Link Project and the Polavaram Irrigation Project.</li>
<li><b>Structural Barriers:</b> presumptive titling, extreme land fragmentation, and high transaction costs under the Act continue to stall infrastructure projects and fuel litigation.</li>
<li><b>Real-World Delays:</b> the Mumbai-Ahmedabad Bullet Train project faced major delays and cost overruns largely due to difficulties acquiring private land under the Act's stringent provisions.</li>
<li><b>Diluted Community Safeguards:</b> despite being a progressive law on paper, poor implementation and weakened community safeguards (Gram Sabha consultation, PESA/FRA compliance) have limited the Act's real-world effectiveness.</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Vision IAS — "Report on Implementation and Effectiveness of the RFCTLARR Act, 2013"; Drishti IAS — "Unlocking India's Land Potential".</p>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eaf7ec; border:1px solid #a8d5b0; border-left:5px solid #388e3c; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Way Forward</b>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0;">Empowering Gram Sabhas, ensuring strict compliance with PESA and the Forest Rights Act, and strengthening the rehabilitation and oversight mechanisms are the key recommendations to make land acquisition genuinely fair and people-centric.</p>
<p style="margin:12px 0 0 0; font-size:0.78em; color:#7a6a55; font-style:italic;">Source: Vision IAS; Drishti IAS.</p>
</div>
