Reference notes on major international groups/groupings (forums without full treaty-based institutional status) relevant to UPSC GS2 — overview, history, goals, and recent activity/conflict for each. #GS2

<h1 style="background-color:#0f3d3e; color:#ffffff; padding:14px 20px; border-radius:8px; font-size:1.15em; letter-spacing:0.5px; text-align:center;">INTERNATIONAL GROUPS AND GROUPINGS</h1>

<h2 style="background-color:#1c5f60; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">1. G7 (Group of Seven)</h2>

<div style="background-color:#e8f5f4; border:1px solid #b7dedb; border-left:5px solid #0f3d3e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Informal forum of leading industrialised democracies: Canada, France, Germany, Italy, Japan, UK, USA, plus the EU as a non-enumerated member.</li>
<li>No treaty, no permanent secretariat or bureaucracy — presidency rotates annually among members, who host and lead discussions in turn.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>History</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Formed 1975 as the G6 (US, UK, France, West Germany, Japan, Italy) in response to the 1973 oil crisis and financial turmoil.</li>
<li>Canada joined 1976 → G7. Russia joined 1998 → G8. Russia expelled 2014 after the annexation of Crimea → reverted to G7.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Goals/Ambition</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Originally: coordinate economic policy among leading industrialised nations.</li>
<li>Now broadened to security, technology, climate change and global governance issues; decisions taken by consensus, not binding.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #edc4bd; border-left:5px solid #b23a2e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:24px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Recent Activity/Conflict</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Coordinated sanctions and Ukraine support since 2022, but unity strained by tariff/trade disputes among members themselves.</li>
<li>Criticised as unrepresentative of a multipolar world — no BRICS economy is a member despite China/India's GDP size.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#1c5f60; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">2. G20 (Group of Twenty)</h2>

<div style="background-color:#e8f5f4; border:1px solid #b7dedb; border-left:5px solid #0f3d3e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Premier forum for international economic cooperation — 19 countries + EU + African Union (since Sept 2023).</li>
<li>Members represent over 80% of world GDP, 75% of international trade, and 60% of world population.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>History</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Created 1999 as a Finance Ministers/Central Bank Governors forum, after the 1997–98 Asian financial crisis.</li>
<li>Upgraded to a Leaders' Summit in 2008 during the global financial crisis, declared the premier platform for international economic cooperation.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Goals/Ambition</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>International financial stability, climate change mitigation, sustainable development; work expanded from economics into digital cooperation and global governance.</li>
<li>Functions via a "troika" of past, current, and next presidency; no permanent secretariat, presidency rotates annually.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #edc4bd; border-left:5px solid #b23a2e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:24px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Recent Activity/Conflict</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>India's 2023 presidency (theme: "One Earth, One Family, One Future") secured permanent African Union membership — a landmark widening of the table.</li>
<li>2023–25: consensus repeatedly strained over exact wording on the Russia–Ukraine war in joint declarations.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#1c5f60; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">3. BRICS</h2>

<div style="background-color:#e8f5f4; border:1px solid #b7dedb; border-left:5px solid #0f3d3e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Grouping of major emerging economies now covering 45% of world population and 37.3% of global GDP (PPP) — bigger than the G7's 29.3% share.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>History</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Acronym "BRIC" coined 2001 by Goldman Sachs economist Jim O'Neill; first informal meeting of Brazil/Russia/India/China 2006 on G8 sidelines.</li>
<li>Inaugural summit: Yekaterinburg, Russia, 2009. South Africa joined 2010 → BRICS.</li>
<li>2024 expansion: Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, UAE became full members (Saudi Arabia invited but not formalised; Argentina opted out). Indonesia joined January 2025.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Goals/Ambition</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Reform global governance/Bretton Woods institutions, deepen South-South cooperation, reduce dependence on Western-led financial architecture.</li>
<li>Key institutions built: New Development Bank (NDB, 2014, HQ Shanghai) and Contingent Reserve Arrangement (CRA) — alternatives to World Bank/IMF.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #edc4bd; border-left:5px solid #b23a2e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:24px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Recent Activity/Conflict</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Growing "de-dollarization" push (local-currency trade, BRICS Pay discussions) has drawn US tariff threats against the bloc.</li>
<li>Internal tension: India wary of the grouping being used as a China-led anti-West platform; India-China rivalry complicates bloc cohesion.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#1c5f60; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">4. G4 (Group of Four)</h2>

<div style="background-color:#e8f5f4; border:1px solid #b7dedb; border-left:5px solid #0f3d3e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>India, Brazil, Germany, Japan — mutually endorsing each other's bids for permanent UNSC membership.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>History</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Formed 2005 specifically to jointly push UN Security Council reform.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Goals/Ambition</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Expand UNSC from 15 to 25–26 members: 6 new permanent + 4–5 new non-permanent seats, with flexibility on new members holding veto power immediately.</li>
<li>Argument: UNSC still reflects 1945 power structure; Africa and Latin America have no permanent representation at all.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #edc4bd; border-left:5px solid #b23a2e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:24px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Recent Activity/Conflict</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Opposed by the "Uniting for Consensus" ("Coffee Club") bloc — Italy, Pakistan, Mexico, Argentina, South Korea — which rejects new permanent seats altogether.</li>
<li>Reform remains stalled as of 2026; needs ratification by all 5 existing P5 members, who show little urgency.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#1c5f60; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">5. NAM (Non-Aligned Movement)</h2>

<div style="background-color:#e8f5f4; border:1px solid #b7dedb; border-left:5px solid #0f3d3e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>121 member states choosing not to formally align with any major Cold War-era power bloc — largest grouping after the UN itself, ~55% of world population.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>History</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Conceptual roots in the 1955 Bandung (Asia-Africa) Conference; formally established 1961 at Belgrade.</li>
<li>Shaped by Nehru (India), Tito (Yugoslavia), Nasser (Egypt), Sukarno (Indonesia), Nkrumah (Ghana) — NAM's "founding fathers".</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Goals/Ambition</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Protect sovereignty/territorial integrity against major-power domination; reduce global tensions; reform the UN for fairer Global South representation.</li>
<li>Guiding principles drawn from Panchsheel (Five Principles of Peaceful Coexistence, India-China, 1954).</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #edc4bd; border-left:5px solid #b23a2e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:24px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Recent Activity/Conflict</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Ongoing relevance debate in today's multipolar (not bipolar) world — the "2012 Non-Alignment 2.0" strategy paper argued for updating the doctrine into "strategic autonomy".</li>
<li>India increasingly practises "multi-alignment" (Quad + SCO + BRICS + I2U2 simultaneously) rather than classic non-alignment, raising questions about NAM's continued centrality to Indian foreign policy.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#1c5f60; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">6. SCO (Shanghai Cooperation Organisation)</h2>

<div style="background-color:#e8f5f4; border:1px solid #b7dedb; border-left:5px solid #0f3d3e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Eurasian political, security and economic bloc; 40%+ of world population, over $23 trillion combined GDP.</li>
<li>Members: China, Russia, India, Pakistan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Uzbekistan, Iran, Belarus.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>History</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Evolved from the "Shanghai Five" (1996: China, Russia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan).</li>
<li>Founded 15 June 2001 (Uzbekistan added); Charter adopted 2002, effective 2003. India and Pakistan joined 2017; Iran 2023; Belarus 2024.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Goals/Ambition</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Mutual trust and security cooperation, counter-terrorism, trade/economic cooperation, push toward a "multipolar" international order.</li>
<li>Structure: Secretariat in Beijing; Regional Anti-Terrorist Structure (RATS) headquartered in Tashkent.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #edc4bd; border-left:5px solid #b23a2e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:24px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Recent Activity/Conflict</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>India-Pakistan and India-China tensions repeatedly complicate consensus — e.g. India has objected when joint statements omit or soft-pedal terror attacks against India.</li>
<li>China-Russia dominance of the grouping's agenda raises concern that SCO functions as a China-centred bloc rather than a neutral platform.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#1c5f60; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">7. QUAD (Quadrilateral Security Dialogue)</h2>

<div style="background-color:#e8f5f4; border:1px solid #b7dedb; border-left:5px solid #0f3d3e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Strategic forum of the US, Japan, India, and Australia — a loose grouping, not a formal alliance like NATO.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>History</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Roots in coordinated relief after the 2004 Indian Ocean tsunami; idea formally floated by Japanese PM Shinzo Abe in 2007 — Australia withdrew soon after, reportedly under Chinese pressure.</li>
<li>Revived 2017; first virtual leaders' summit March 2021, first in-person summit September 2021.</li>
<li>Military dimension: the Malabar naval exercise (India-US since 1992) added Japan (2015) and Australia (2020).</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Goals/Ambition</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Free and open Indo-Pacific, rules-based order, freedom of navigation — implicitly to balance China's regional influence.</li>
<li>Works via 6 thematic working groups: health, climate, critical & emerging tech, space, infrastructure, cybersecurity. No secretariat or treaty.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #edc4bd; border-left:5px solid #b23a2e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:24px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Recent Activity/Conflict</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>China routinely denounces the Quad as an "Asian NATO" / containment strategy.</li>
<li>Summit scheduling has faced uncertainty amid shifting US administration priorities, raising questions about the grouping's institutional durability compared to treaty alliances.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#1c5f60; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">8. ASEAN (Association of Southeast Asian Nations)</h2>

<div style="background-color:#e8f5f4; border:1px solid #b7dedb; border-left:5px solid #0f3d3e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>11-member Southeast Asian regional grouping; motto "One Vision, One Identity, One Community". India is not a member but a key Dialogue Partner.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>History</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Founded 1967 via the Bangkok Declaration (Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines, Singapore, Thailand); grew to 11 members (Brunei, Vietnam, Myanmar, Laos, Cambodia, Timor-Leste).</li>
<li>Key milestones: Treaty of Amity and Cooperation (1976), ASEAN Free Trade Area (1992), ASEAN Economic Community (2015).</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Goals/Ambition</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Regional peace/security and economic-cultural cooperation, guided by consensus and non-interference ("the ASEAN Way").</li>
<li>India-ASEAN ties: Sectoral Partner (1992) → Dialogue Partner (1996) → Summit-level Partner (2002) → Strategic Partnership (2012) → Comprehensive Strategic Partnership (2026); the institutional foundation of India's Act East Policy.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #edc4bd; border-left:5px solid #b23a2e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:24px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Recent Activity/Conflict</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>South China Sea disputes (China vs. Philippines/Vietnam) repeatedly test ASEAN's consensus-based unity.</li>
<li>Myanmar's post-2021 civil war has strained the "non-interference" principle, with member states divided on how to engage the military junta.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#1c5f60; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">9. G77 (Group of 77)</h2>

<div style="background-color:#e8f5f4; border:1px solid #b7dedb; border-left:5px solid #0f3d3e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Largest intergovernmental grouping of developing countries at the UN — 134+ members; China participates as a supporting "partner", not a formal member.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>History</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Established 15 June 1964 via the "Joint Declaration of the Seventy-Seven Developing Countries" at the first UNCTAD session, Geneva.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Goals/Ambition</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Give the Global South a unified negotiating voice on economic issues within the UN system.</li>
<li>Structure: chapters in Geneva (UN), Rome (FAO), Vienna (UNIDO), Paris (UNESCO), Nairobi (UNEP), plus the "Group of 24" in Washington D.C. for IMF/World Bank issues. South Summit = supreme decision-making body.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #edc4bd; border-left:5px solid #b23a2e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:24px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Recent Activity/Conflict</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Overlaps and sometimes competes with India's own "Voice of Global South" summits (2023–24) for leadership of Global South advocacy.</li>
<li>Recurring friction with developed nations over climate finance/"loss and damage" funding at COP negotiations.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#1c5f60; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">10. I2U2 (India–Israel–UAE–US)</h2>

<div style="background-color:#e8f5f4; border:1px solid #b7dedb; border-left:5px solid #0f3d3e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Strategic economic partnership nicknamed the "West Asian Quad"/"Middle East Quad" — 'I2' = India + Israel, 'U2' = US + UAE.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>History</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Platform established October 2021, following the 2020 Abraham Accords (Israel-UAE normalisation); first virtual leaders' summit July 2022.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Goals/Ambition</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Joint investment across six identified areas: water, energy, transportation, space, health, food security.</li>
<li>No secretariat — functions purely through summits; e.g. UAE pledged $2 billion for Indian food-park development.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #edc4bd; border-left:5px solid #b23a2e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:24px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Recent Activity/Conflict</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>The Israel-Gaza war (2023 onward) has tested I2U2's political viability given Israel's contested regional standing, even as economic projects have continued.</li>
<li>Illustrates India's "multi-alignment": India sits in SCO, Quad, and I2U2 simultaneously — a modern extension of strategic autonomy.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#1c5f60; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">11. NSG (Nuclear Suppliers Group)</h2>

<div style="background-color:#e8f5f4; border:1px solid #b7dedb; border-left:5px solid #0f3d3e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Club of ~48 nuclear-supplier countries that controls exports of nuclear material, equipment, and technology, to prevent proliferation while allowing peaceful nuclear trade.</li>
<li>Not a treaty body — a voluntary supplier cartel with no legal enforcement power; guidelines are implemented through each member's own export-control laws.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>History</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Formed 1974, directly in response to India's first nuclear test ("Smiling Buddha") — founding members wanted to close the loophole that let India build a weapon using material/technology originally supplied for peaceful purposes.</li>
<li>Formal guidelines adopted 1978.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Goals/Ambition</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Restrict nuclear-related exports to countries that meet non-proliferation safeguards/guidelines; decisions taken purely by <b>consensus</b> — any single member can block an outcome.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #edc4bd; border-left:5px solid #b23a2e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:24px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Recent Activity/Conflict</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>2008 "India-specific waiver" (heavily lobbied by the US as part of the India-US nuclear deal) let India resume civil nuclear trade despite staying outside the NPT — a one-off trading exception, not membership.</li>
<li>India's separate bid for <b>full NSG membership</b> (a seat in setting the rules, not just an exception to them) remains blocked, chiefly by <b>China</b> — which argues any exception for India should extend to Pakistan too, and objects to non-NPT states joining at all. Pakistan has a parallel, equally stalled bid.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#1c5f60; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">12. SAARC (South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation)</h2>

<div style="background-color:#e8f5f4; border:1px solid #b7dedb; border-left:5px solid #0f3d3e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Regional intergovernmental grouping of 8 South Asian states: India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Nepal, Bhutan, Maldives, and Afghanistan (joined 2007).</li>
<li>India accounts for ~70% of SAARC's population and ~80% of its collective GDP — the same "big neighbour" asymmetry problem covered in [[Pax Indica]] §3.1/§3.9.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>History</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Founded 1985 in Dhaka; Secretariat based in Kathmandu, Nepal.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Goals/Ambition</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Promote economic, social, and cultural cooperation among South Asian states — trade integration (the South Asian Free Trade Area/SAFTA, signed 2004), connectivity, and poverty alleviation.</li>
<li>Decisions require unanimous consensus, and bilateral disputes between members are explicitly excluded from the agenda — a rule meant to keep the forum neutral, but which in practice shields the India-Pakistan relationship from ever being addressed at SAARC.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #edc4bd; border-left:5px solid #b23a2e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:24px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Recent Activity/Conflict</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Effectively dormant since <b>2016</b> — India (joined by Bangladesh, Bhutan, Afghanistan) boycotted the scheduled Islamabad summit after the Uri terror attack. No SAARC summit has been held since.</li>
<li>India now prioritises <b>BIMSTEC</b> (Bay of Bengal grouping, which excludes Pakistan) as its preferred South Asian regional forum instead.</li>
<li>SAFTA has never matured into a genuine customs union — Pakistan's intransigence and the India-dominance concern repeatedly block deeper trade integration.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#1c5f60; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">13. BIMSTEC (Bay of Bengal Initiative for Multi-Sectoral Technical and Economic Cooperation)</h2>

<div style="background-color:#e8f5f4; border:1px solid #b7dedb; border-left:5px solid #0f3d3e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>7-member regional grouping bridging South Asia and Southeast Asia around the Bay of Bengal: Bangladesh, India, Myanmar, Sri Lanka, Thailand, Nepal, Bhutan.</li>
<li>Notably excludes Pakistan — India's preferred alternative to SAARC since the latter's 2016 breakdown (see SAARC above).</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>History</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Founded 1997 as "BIST-EC" (Bangladesh, India, Sri Lanka, Thailand Economic Cooperation); renamed BIMSTEC the same year when Myanmar joined. Nepal and Bhutan joined in 2004.</li>
<li>Secretariat established in Dhaka, Bangladesh, in 2014.</li>
<li>A formal BIMSTEC Charter — giving the grouping legal personality — was adopted at the 2022 Colombo Summit, 25 years after founding.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Goals/Ambition</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Cooperation across 14 sectors including trade, connectivity, energy, counter-terrorism, tourism, fisheries, and agriculture — envisioned as a bridge between SAARC and ASEAN economic spaces.</li>
<li>Key initiatives: the BIMSTEC Free Trade Area Framework Agreement (signed 2004, still not fully implemented) and a Master Plan for Transport Connectivity.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #edc4bd; border-left:5px solid #b23a2e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:24px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Recent Activity/Conflict</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Gained sudden prominence in 2016 when India invited BIMSTEC leaders (instead of SAARC's) to the BRICS Goa Summit outreach session, signalling its shift away from a Pakistan-inclusive regional forum.</li>
<li>Conducted its first joint military exercise, MILEX, in 2018 — a notable step toward the security cooperation SAARC never achieved.</li>
<li>Still criticised for slow implementation: the 2004 FTA framework remains largely unrealised two decades on, echoing SAARC's own follow-through problems.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#1c5f60; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">14. IOR-ARC / IORA (Indian Ocean Rim Association)</h2>

<div style="background-color:#e8f5f4; border:1px solid #b7dedb; border-left:5px solid #0f3d3e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Regional forum of 23 Indian Ocean littoral states + 9 dialogue partners — the only grouping dedicated exclusively to Indian Ocean regional cooperation.</li>
<li>Originally named IOR-ARC (Indian Ocean Rim Association for Regional Cooperation); renamed simply <b>IORA</b> in 2013 to reflect a broader mandate beyond regional cooperation. HQ: Port Louis, <b>Mauritius</b>.</li>
<li>Members span East Africa, Gulf, South Asia, Southeast Asia and Australasia — giving IORA a uniquely diverse, cross-continental character.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>History</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Idea conceptualised in early 1990s; founded <b>1997</b> in Port Louis — the same year as BIMSTEC — with 14 original member states including India as a founding member.</li>
<li>India and Mauritius were the principal architects; South Africa also played a central role in the early years.</li>
<li>Shashi Tharoor (in <i>Pax Indica</i>, Chapter 5) calls IOR-ARC India's "most underappreciated and neglected grouping" — India's engagement had historically been sporadic despite its obvious strategic salience.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Goals/Ambition</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Promote a <b>free, open, and prosperous Indian Ocean region</b>; focus areas: maritime security, trade facilitation, fisheries management, disaster risk management, academic/science cooperation, tourism, blue economy, and women's economic empowerment.</li>
<li>Structure: Council of Ministers (apex), Committee of Senior Officials, Working Groups per sector; functions by consensus, no enforcement mechanism.</li>
<li>Strategic salience: the Indian Ocean carries ~<b>80% of the world's seaborne oil trade</b> and over half of all container traffic — control and cooperation here directly affects global supply chains.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #edc4bd; border-left:5px solid #b23a2e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:24px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Recent Activity/Conflict</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>India has since 2015 re-energised its Indian Ocean diplomacy through the <b>SAGAR doctrine</b> (Security And Growth for All in the Region — announced by PM Modi in Mauritius 2015) and the <b>MAHASAGAR</b> (Mutual and Holistic Advancement for Security and Growth Across Regions) vision — IORA is the institutional vehicle for both.</li>
<li>2017 Jakarta IORA Leaders' Summit (India hosted the special Summit) adopted the Jakarta Concord — IORA's first comprehensive strategic document — covering blue economy, maritime safety, and trade/investment facilitation.</li>
<li>China is not a member (as a non-littoral state it cannot join), but is a dialogue partner — a structural limit that gives IORA potential as a China-neutral Indian Ocean framework, a role India has been slow to leverage fully.</li>
<li>Piracy in the Gulf of Aden, Houthi attacks on Red Sea shipping (2023–25), and growing Chinese naval presence in the Indian Ocean have made IORA's maritime security mandate far more urgent than it was at founding.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:24px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>UPSC GS2 Linkage</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Core to "Important International institutions, agencies and fora, their structure, mandate" — a named GS2 syllabus topic.</li>
<li>Good comparison-table material: which groups are India a founding/full member of (G20, G4, SCO, Quad, I2U2, NAM, G77, SAARC, BIMSTEC, IORA) vs. dialogue partner only (ASEAN) vs. non-member (G7) vs. blocked-membership-bid (NSG).</li>
<li>Recurring PYQ pattern: "Do you think [X grouping] has lost relevance / can effectively address [Y global challenge]?" — use the Recent Activity/Conflict points above as the critical/analytical spine of such answers.</li>
</ul>
</div>
