Reference notes on major formal international organisations (treaty-based bodies with permanent structures) relevant to UPSC GS2 — overview, history, governance structure/dominant countries/leadership, and recent activity/conflict for each. #GS2

<h1 style="background-color:#2c1f52; color:#ffffff; padding:14px 20px; border-radius:8px; font-size:1.15em; letter-spacing:0.5px; text-align:center;">INTERNATIONAL ORGANISATIONS</h1>

<h2 style="background-color:#3d2d70; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">1. United Nations (UN)</h2>

<div style="background-color:#f0edf9; border:1px solid #cfc4ea; border-left:5px solid #2c1f52; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>193-member global body for international peace, security, and cooperation — successor to the failed League of Nations.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>History</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Idea traced to the 1941 Atlantic Charter (US-UK); founded 1945 when 26 Allied nations signed the "Declaration by United Nations".</li>
<li>UN Charter signed at the San Francisco Conference, 1945; UNGA's first session convened 10 January 1946, London, with 51 nations.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eef1f5; border:1px solid #c7d2de; border-left:5px solid #3d5a80; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Structure &amp; Governance</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>6 principal organs:</b> UN General Assembly (UNGA), Security Council (UNSC), Economic and Social Council (ECOSOC), Trusteeship Council (inactive since decolonisation completed, 1994), International Court of Justice (ICJ, The Hague), and the Secretariat.</li>
<li><b>UNGA:</b> all 193 members, one-nation-one-vote, main policy-making/deliberative body — but resolutions are non-binding recommendations.</li>
<li><b>UNSC:</b> 15 members — 5 permanent (<b>P5: US, UK, France, Russia, China</b>) holding veto power, plus 10 non-permanent members elected by UNGA for 2-year terms (5 replaced annually). Sole body with binding enforcement authority on peace/security.</li>
<li><b>ICJ:</b> 15 judges, principal judicial organ, settles disputes between states.</li>
<li><b>Secretariat:</b> headed by the Secretary-General, the chief administrative officer.</li>
<li><b>Dominant countries:</b> the P5's veto gives them structurally outsized power over UNGA's formal equality; funding-wise the US is the largest contributor to the regular budget (~22%), followed by China, Japan, Germany.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #edc4bd; border-left:5px solid #b23a2e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:24px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Recent Activity/Conflict</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Veto deadlock: Russia has cast ~19 vetoes since 2011 (mostly Syria, also Ukraine); the US vetoed a 2023 resolution on Gaza humanitarian pauses — UNGA's president has called the UNSC "paralysed" and "dysfunctional" since the Russia-Ukraine war began.</li>
<li>Reform proposals include the G4 model (expand to 25–26 seats) and a France-Mexico proposal to ban veto use in genocide/mass-atrocity cases — both stalled.</li>
<li>"Summit of the Future" (2024) and "UN80" initiative are ongoing efforts to revitalise the institution amid a funding crisis driven partly by US arrears/withdrawal moves from linked agencies.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#3d2d70; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">2. World Bank (Group)</h2>

<div style="background-color:#f0edf9; border:1px solid #cfc4ea; border-left:5px solid #2c1f52; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Bretton Woods institution for development finance and poverty reduction — a "World Bank Group" of 5 sister institutions, not a single body.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>History</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Emerged from the July 1944 Bretton Woods Conference; IBRD Articles of Agreement ratified December 1945; began operations June 1946.</li>
<li>Initial mission: post-WWII European reconstruction; mission shifted over decades toward poverty reduction, infrastructure, human capital, and institutional reform in developing countries.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eef1f5; border:1px solid #c7d2de; border-left:5px solid #3d5a80; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Structure &amp; Governance</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>5 institutions:</b> IBRD (International Bank for Reconstruction and Development), IDA (International Development Association, concessional loans to poorest countries), IFC (International Finance Corporation, private-sector arm), MIGA (Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency), ICSID (investment dispute arbitration).</li>
<li><b>Board of Governors</b> (all member states) = highest decision body; <b>Board of 25 Executive Directors</b> = day-to-day oversight.</li>
<li>By long-standing convention, the <b>President is always a US citizen</b>, nominated by the US as the largest shareholder — currently <b>Ajay Banga</b> (India-born, since 2023).</li>
<li><b>Dominant countries (voting shares):</b> US 16.41% (large enough to block major decisions needing an 85% supermajority — an effective US veto), Japan 7.87%, Germany, UK, France, China among the next largest.</li>
<li>IBRD has 189 members, IDA has 174.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #edc4bd; border-left:5px solid #b23a2e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:24px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Recent Activity/Conflict</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>"Mission 300" — electrify 300 million Africans by 2030 — flagship Banga-era initiative, alongside AgriConnect (agriculture) and healthcare-access expansion for 1.5 billion people.</li>
<li>From January 2026, restructuring into a unified "One World Bank Group" merging IBRD/IDA/IFC knowledge teams into five verticals (People, Prosperity, Planet, Infrastructure, Digital).</li>
<li>Climate finance share targeted to rise from 26% to 35% of total lending — but private climate-adaptation financing remains stagnant, a recurring criticism of the Bank's model.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#3d2d70; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">3. International Monetary Fund (IMF)</h2>

<div style="background-color:#f0edf9; border:1px solid #cfc4ea; border-left:5px solid #2c1f52; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Bretton Woods institution for monetary cooperation, exchange-rate stability, and balance-of-payments support — 191 member countries.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>History</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Also born at the 1944 Bretton Woods Conference (World Bank's "twin" institution); began financial operations in the late 1940s.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eef1f5; border:1px solid #c7d2de; border-left:5px solid #3d5a80; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Structure &amp; Governance</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Board of Governors</b> (all members) = highest body; <b>Executive Board</b> (24 Executive Directors) = day-to-day operations; <b>Managing Director</b> appointed by the Executive Board for a 5-year term — by convention always a European (current MD: <b>Kristalina Georgieva</b>, since Oct 2019); by convention the First Deputy MD is American.</li>
<li><b>Quota system:</b> each member's financial contribution, which sets both voting power and borrowing access, is pegged to the size of its economy.</li>
<li><b>Dominant countries:</b> the US holds the largest quota (enough for an effective veto since major decisions need an 85% majority), followed by Japan, China, Germany, France, UK. India is the 13th-largest quota holder (~2.6–2.75% share).</li>
<li>The 2010 quota/governance reform only took effect in 2016 (delayed by US Congress reluctance) and raised India's voting share from 2.3% to 2.6% — a modest realignment relative to India's actual economic growth.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #edc4bd; border-left:5px solid #b23a2e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:24px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Recent Activity/Conflict</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>India abstained from/objected to a 2025 IMF loan of ~$2.3 billion to Pakistan, citing concerns the funds could be misused given Pakistan's terror-financing track record.</li>
<li>Global South continues pushing for further quota realignment to reflect the growing economic weight of emerging economies — progress remains slow.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#3d2d70; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">4. World Health Organization (WHO)</h2>

<div style="background-color:#f0edf9; border:1px solid #cfc4ea; border-left:5px solid #2c1f52; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>UN specialized agency for global public health — 194 member states, HQ Geneva.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>History</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Established 7 April 1948 — the date is now marked annually as World Health Day.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eef1f5; border:1px solid #c7d2de; border-left:5px solid #3d5a80; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Structure &amp; Governance</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>World Health Assembly (WHA)</b> = supreme decision body, all member states, meets annually in Geneva; <b>Executive Board</b> = health specialists elected for 3-year terms; <b>Secretariat</b> led by the Director-General (nominated by the Executive Board, appointed by the WHA).</li>
<li>6 regional offices — e.g. South-East Asia Regional Office (SEARO) in New Delhi covers India.</li>
<li><b>Funding:</b> only ~16–20% comes from mandatory GDP-based "assessed" contributions; the rest is voluntary — top voluntary donors historically include the US, Germany, UK, and the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation.</li>
<li>Current Director-General: <b>Dr Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus</b> (Ethiopia) — WHO's first African DG.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #edc4bd; border-left:5px solid #b23a2e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:24px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Recent Activity/Conflict</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>The US (~20% of WHO's funding) announced withdrawal via a January 2025 executive order — citing COVID-19 mishandling and alleged political bias — with over $270 million in unpaid arrears; withdrawal takes effect after the mandatory 1-year notice period.</li>
<li>The first global Pandemic Agreement was adopted in May 2025 to strengthen pandemic preparedness/equitable vaccine access — but US negotiators withdrew from the process, weakening its impact given the US dominates global vaccine production.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#3d2d70; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">5. World Trade Organization (WTO)</h2>

<div style="background-color:#f0edf9; border:1px solid #cfc4ea; border-left:5px solid #2c1f52; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Sole global body governing rules of trade between nations; unlike IMF/WB, formally operates on one-country-one-vote consensus.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>History</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Successor to GATT (General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade, 1947); created via the Uruguay Round (1986–94) and the 1994 Marrakesh Agreement.</li>
<li>Began operations 1 January 1995.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eef1f5; border:1px solid #c7d2de; border-left:5px solid #3d5a80; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Structure &amp; Governance</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Ministerial Conference</b> = top decision body (trade ministers of all members), meets every 2 years; <b>General Council</b> (Geneva) = highest day-to-day body, which also convenes as the <b>Dispute Settlement Body</b> and the <b>Trade Policy Review Body</b>.</li>
<li><b>Dispute settlement</b> was designed as 2-tier: panels, then a standing 7-member <b>Appellate Body</b> (est. 1995) for final appeal.</li>
<li>Current Director-General: <b>Ngozi Okonjo-Iweala</b> (Nigeria, since Feb 2021) — WTO's first woman and first African DG.</li>
<li><b>Dominant members:</b> formally equal votes, but the US, EU, and China wield outsized real influence through market size and blocking power — most visibly, the US alone has paralysed the Appellate Body by blocking judge appointments.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #edc4bd; border-left:5px solid #b23a2e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:24px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Recent Activity/Conflict</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Appellate Body has been non-functional since December 2019 — countries can now "appeal into the void", leaving disputes unresolved indefinitely; India has named its restoration the top WTO reform priority.</li>
<li>Rising tariffs/protectionism (2025–26 US-China trade tensions, "national security" justifications for tariffs) are undermining core free-trade principles; global merchandise trade growth projected to slow as a result.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#3d2d70; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">6. UNESCO (UN Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization)</h2>

<div style="background-color:#f0edf9; border:1px solid #cfc4ea; border-left:5px solid #2c1f52; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>UN specialized agency promoting peace through cooperation in education, science, and culture — 194 members + 12 associate members, HQ Paris.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>History</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Founded 16 November 1945, succeeding the earlier International Committee on Intellectual Cooperation; first General Conference held Paris, Nov-Dec 1946.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eef1f5; border:1px solid #c7d2de; border-left:5px solid #3d5a80; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Structure &amp; Governance</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>General Conference</b> (all members, supreme body, meets biennially), an <b>Executive Board</b>, and a <b>Secretariat</b> led by the Director-General.</li>
<li>Five focus areas: education, natural sciences, social/human sciences, culture, communication & information.</li>
<li><b>India:</b> member since UNESCO's inception (1946); represented via the Indian National Commission for Cooperation with UNESCO (INCCU); has 45 World Heritage Sites (as of July 2026 — 37 cultural, 7 natural, 1 mixed).</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #edc4bd; border-left:5px solid #b23a2e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:24px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Recent Activity/Conflict</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>The US has now announced withdrawal for a <b>third time</b> (after exits in 1984 and 2017, and rejoining in 2023) — effective 31 December 2026, citing alleged anti-Israel bias.</li>
<li>This repeated in-out pattern by a major funder is a recurring case study in the fragility of multilateral institution funding.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 style="background-color:#3d2d70; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px; margin-top:20px;">7. International Labour Organization (ILO)</h2>

<div style="background-color:#f0edf9; border:1px solid #cfc4ea; border-left:5px solid #2c1f52; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>UN specialized agency for labour standards and "decent work" — unique for its tripartite structure. 187 member states.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#faf1e6; border:1px solid #ecd6b8; border-left:5px solid #c9701b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>History</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Created 1919 under the Treaty of Versailles (post-WWI), on the premise that lasting peace requires social justice; became a UN specialized agency in 1946.</li>
<li>Awarded the Nobel Peace Prize in 1969.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eef1f5; border:1px solid #c7d2de; border-left:5px solid #3d5a80; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:12px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Structure &amp; Governance</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li><b>Tripartite structure</b> — the only UN body where government, employer, and worker representatives all sit in the decision-making organs, not just governments.</li>
<li><b>International Labour Conference</b> (annual, Geneva — the "international parliament of labour") sets labour standards; <b>Governing Body</b> = executive council (policy/budget decisions); <b>International Labour Office</b> = permanent secretariat, led by the Director-General.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#fbe9e7; border:1px solid #edc4bd; border-left:5px solid #b23a2e; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-bottom:24px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Recent Activity/Conflict</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Recent flagship reports: "World of Work" and "Employment and Social Trends" (2026) track global labour-market shifts; reports on the economics of forced labour highlight persistent exploitation globally.</li>
<li>ILO conventions on freedom of association are a frequent reference point in debates over India's own labour code reforms.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:24px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>UPSC GS2 Linkage</b>
<ul style="margin:6px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li>Directly maps to the GS2 syllabus line: "Important International institutions, agencies and fora — their structure, mandate."</li>
<li>Common answer pattern: every one of these bodies shows the same tension — <b>formal universal membership vs. informal dominance by a handful of powers</b> (US veto-equivalent in IMF/WB, P5 veto in UNSC, US blocking WTO's Appellate Body) — useful as a unifying argument across multiple GS2 questions.</li>
<li>Cross-link to [[Pax Indica]] Chapter 1 (§1.6, "Beyond the Book" box) — the multipolarity argument there is the theoretical frame; this doc supplies the institutional detail.</li>
</ul>
</div>
