<b>Overview</b>
<p style="font-size:0.9em; color:#444444; margin:8px 0 0 0;">The Annual Status of Education Report (ASER) is a household-level survey conducted by the ASER Centre (Pratham), assessing children's foundational reading and arithmetic skills across rural India, along with school enrollment and attendance patterns. Used here as the source for findings on poor foundational learning outcomes among 14–18-year-olds, and the ASER 2023 finding on girls aged 15–18 remaining out of school.</p>
