<b>Overview</b>
<p style="font-size:0.9em; color:#444444; margin:8px 0 0 0;">The main yearly publication of the [[PLFS]] (Periodic Labour Force Survey), released by MoSPI, covering a full survey year (July–June). It gives annual, gender- and sector-disaggregated estimates of LFPR, WPR, Unemployment Rate, and average earnings/wages for rural and urban India. Used here as the source for the Labour Force Participation Rate and casual-labour wage-gap figures.</p>
