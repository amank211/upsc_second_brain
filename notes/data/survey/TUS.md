<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px;">Time Use Survey (TUS)</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<p style="font-size:0.9em; color:#444444; margin:8px 0 0 0;">Conducted by the National Statistical Office (NSO) under MoSPI, it measures how individuals spend their time across a day — paid work, unpaid domestic and care work, learning, leisure, self-care, etc. It's the primary source for quantifying unpaid work, and is especially used to highlight gendered disparities in time spent on domestic and caregiving work. First conducted in 2019, with a second round in 2024.</p>
</div>
