<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px;">Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS)</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<p style="font-size:0.9em; color:#444444; margin:8px 0 0 0;">Conducted by the National Sample Survey Office (NSSO) under the Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI). It provides quarterly estimates (urban areas) and annual estimates (rural + urban) of key employment/unemployment indicators — Labour Force Participation Rate (LFPR), Worker Population Ratio (WPR), Unemployment Rate, and wage/earnings data — broken down by gender, sector, and employment type. Launched in 2017, replacing the earlier quinquennial NSSO employment-unemployment surveys.</p>
</div>
