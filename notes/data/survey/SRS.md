<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px;">Sample Registration System (SRS)</h2>

<div style="background-color:#eef4f3; border:1px solid #bcd4cf; border-left:5px solid #2c7a6b; border-radius:6px; padding:12px 16px; margin-top:16px; width:100%; box-sizing:border-box;">
<b>Overview</b>
<p style="font-size:0.9em; color:#444444; margin:8px 0 0 0;">A large-scale demographic survey run by the Office of the Registrar General of India (RGI), Ministry of Home Affairs. It uses a dual-record system — continuous local registration of births/deaths plus an independent periodic retrospective survey — to produce reliable annual estimates of the birth rate, death rate, infant mortality rate, maternal mortality ratio (MMR), and sex ratio at birth (SRB) for India and its states.</p>
