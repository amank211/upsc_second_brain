<h2 style="background-color:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:10px 16px; border-radius:6px;">Children — Key Data</h2>

<p style="font-size:1.1em; color:#6b6b6b; margin:20px 0 10px 0; font-weight:800; text-transform:uppercase; letter-spacing:1px;">Demographics</p>
<div style="display:flex; flex-direction:column; gap:12px;">

<div style="font-size:1.6em; font-weight:bold; color:#2c7a6b; margin-top:4px;">39%</div>
<p style="font-size:0.88em; color:#555555; margin:6px 0 0 0;">Children account for 39% of India's population, making them one of the largest demographic groups and a key focus for welfare and protection policy.</p>
<p style="font-size:0.72em; color:#aaaaaa; margin:8px 0 0 0; font-style:italic;">Source: Census 2011</p>

<div style="font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em;">School Dropouts <span style="font-weight:400; color:#999999; font-size:0.8em;">(2023–24)</span></div>
<div style="font-size:1.6em; font-weight:bold; color:#2c7a6b; margin-top:4px;">1.72 crore</div>
<p style="font-size:0.88em; color:#555555; margin:6px 0 0 0;">1.72 crore children dropped out, as school enrolment fell from 26.5 crore in 2021–22 to 24.8 crore in 2023–24.</p>

<p style="font-size:1.1em; color:#6b6b6b; margin:24px 0 10px 0; font-weight:800; text-transform:uppercase; letter-spacing:1px;">Abuse &amp; Exploitation</p>
<div style="display:flex; flex-direction:column; gap:12px;">

<div style="background-color:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e2e2e2; border-top:4px solid #c0392b; border-radius:8px; padding:14px;">
<div style="font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em;">Crimes Against Children <span style="font-weight:400; color:#999999; font-size:0.8em;">(2022)</span></div>
<div style="font-size:1.6em; font-weight:bold; color:#c0392b; margin-top:4px;">18 / hour</div>
<p style="font-size:0.88em; color:#555555; margin:6px 0 0 0;">18 crimes against children occur every hour, with one-third falling under the POCSO Act. Family members or acquaintances are often the perpetrators.</p>

<div style="background-color:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e2e2e2; border-top:4px solid #c0392b; border-radius:8px; padding:14px;">
<div style="font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em;">Child Labor</div>
<div style="font-size:1.6em; font-weight:bold; color:#c0392b; margin-top:4px;">10 million+</div>
<p style="font-size:0.88em; color:#555555; margin:6px 0 0 0;">India has the largest number of working children globally, with over 10 million children aged 5–14 involved in child labor.</p>
<p style="font-size:0.72em; color:#aaaaaa; margin:8px 0 0 0; font-style:italic;">Source: as provided (not independently verified this session)</p>

<p style="font-size:0.88em; color:#555555; margin:6px 0 0 0;">India has one of the highest Infant Mortality Rates at 34 per 1,000 live births; diarrhea and malnutrition are primary causes of death for children under five.</p>
